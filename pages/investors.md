## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

CodersTrust is building JobReady, an AI-native digital education and employment platform for emerging markets, on a track record of 130,000+ learners trained since 2014.

The Thesis

## Skills and Work Are Not Connecting.

Educated young people are not in work or training, and employers cannot find skills. Courses that sell content do not close the gap, and AI is raising the bar for entry work.

### Educated Young People Are Not in Work or Training; Employers Cannot Find Skills.

62 million

estimated young people aged 15–24 with higher-secondary education or more not in employment, education or training across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa (range 61–78 million)

72%

of employers globally report difficulty finding talent

Separately, 19M+ university-educated people of all working ages (15 and over) are unemployed across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) and Sub-Saharan Africa, a floor figure rather than a youth figure.

### Online Learning Sells Content, Not Earnings.

Many online courses provide content without the work-entry support that turns skills into income: mentoring, certification and placement.

### AI Is Raising the Bar for Entry Work.

Globally, jobs requiring AI skills grew 69% vs. 9% for all jobs, with a 62% wage premium

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

Every trained young person who remains out of work leaves that gap open.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

Where We Stand

For impact investors weighing a platform for emerging-market youth employment, CodersTrust offers JobReady, a platform rolling out in stages on top of its training and mentoring programs. Its results are measured, reported and open to independent evaluation. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the path from training toward paid work, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer and gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are vulnerable to automation, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done. Her Power, a national program of DoICT, the ICT Division, trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas). CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly. Six government contracts also have training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff. Separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs run hub-and-spoke: up to 10 cohorts in parallel, with certified local facilitators and blended delivery in existing labs and colleges. In Bangladesh, 9,000+ government digital labs could host blended learning; lab access and seats are confirmed per program. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we plan phase by phase; we have not yet delivered one at that size.

### Is There Independent Evidence?

Yes — a randomized controlled trial by BIGD (BRAC University) of CodersTrust’s WSDFM program studied women in Dhaka trained in 2020, on a 200-hour predecessor of today’s 100-hour track. It found that women offered a place were more likely to be employed than the control group. The trial surveyed 599 women at follow-up (report published May 2022). For context, a review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.
