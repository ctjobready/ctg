Starting Point

A trainee from the first batch of the WSDFM project, from a middle-income family in Bangladesh.

What They Did

During the training period, she began offering her services as a graphic designer on the freelance marketplace Fiverr.

Where They Are Now

She continues to work as a freelancer and has completed 90+ projects on Fiverr so far.
