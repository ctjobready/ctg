## Questions About This Course

### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

Build a foundation in cryptography, network security, digital forensics and cyber risk management, then apply it in the final capstone project or internship. Your next step depends on your experience, portfolio and local market.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. Choose Cyber Security if you want to study cryptography, network security, digital forensics and cyber law. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
