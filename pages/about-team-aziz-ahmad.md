# Aziz Ahmad

Co-founder & Chairman

- Future of Work

Aziz Ahmad is the Co-Founder and Chairman of CodersTrust, which he founded in 2014. A Bangladeshi-American business leader, he is passionate about making applied education affordable and accessible to the masses. His goal is to empower people to break through traditional career barriers and acquire the modern digital skills needed to meet global skill demands, so that they can earn a living.

Together with co-founder Ferdinand Kjærulff, a former Danish military captain, he launched CodersTrust in Copenhagen in 2014, in the presence of Sir Richard Branson and investor Morten Lund.

An award-winning entrepreneur, he is a sought-after speaker at forums including the US Senate, the World Economic Forum, the European Parliament, and Vatican City, where he speaks about the Future of Work and the Common Good in the Digital Age. He received the NOVA Award in 2022 for his contribution to the green technology industry and chairs the US-Bangladesh Global Chamber of Commerce.
