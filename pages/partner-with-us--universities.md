How It Works

## JobReady@Campus: Training Alongside the Degree

Our Approach

For universities and colleges that need to prepare their degree holders for paid digital work, CodersTrust offers an integrated training-to-earnings model that runs alongside the degree. We combine training, mentoring and placement support in existing labs and colleges. Partners receive monthly reports on progress toward paid work.

### Campus Labs as Learning Centers

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

Employment at follow-up: 52.8% in the control group and 63.1% for women offered a place.

## Questions Universities Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand for your graduates’ skills and on your campus labs, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### What Happens After the First Cohort?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays on your campus: certified local facilitators, localized curricula and campus labs that serve as blended-learning centers. Completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring, and TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so they can keep advancing.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a partnership includes.
