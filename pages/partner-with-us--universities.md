How It Works

## JobReady@Campus: Training Alongside the Degree

Our Approach

For universities and colleges that need to prepare their degree holders for paid digital work, CodersTrust offers an integrated training-to-earnings model that runs alongside the degree. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the path from training toward paid work, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.
- Set Up

### Campus Labs as Learning Centers

On-campus labs become blended-learning centers with certified local facilitators; faculty members act as coordinators.
- Prepare for Certification

### Industry Certification Tracks

100 training hours per certification, with certification exam preparation; exam eligibility, attempts and terms are agreed in the program scope.
- Mentor and Place

### Mentoring and Placement

Three months of milestone-based mentoring and placement support, with outcome tracking for 12 months.
- Alongside Studies

### In Parallel with the Degree

Training runs in parallel with academic studies, with professional internships where employer placements are available.

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

## Questions Universities Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand for your graduates’ skills and on your campus labs, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it. We set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis. CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories.

### What Happens After the First Cohort?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays on your campus: certified local facilitators, localized curricula and campus labs that serve as blended-learning centers. Completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring, and TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so they can keep advancing.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.
