How It Works

## JobReady@Campus: Training Alongside the Degree

Our Approach

JobReady@Campus combines training, mentoring and placement support in campus labs alongside academic studies. Your institution receives monthly reports on progress toward paid work, as agreed in the program scope.
- Set Up

### Campus Labs as Learning Centers

On-campus labs become blended-learning centers with certified local facilitators; faculty members act as coordinators.
- Prepare for Certification

### Industry Certification Tracks

100 training hours per certification, with exam preparation; eligibility, attempts and terms are agreed in the program scope.
- Mentor and Place

### Mentoring and Placement

Three months of milestone-based mentoring and placement support, with outcome tracking for 12 months after each course, as agreed in the program scope.
- Alongside Studies

### In Parallel with the Degree

Training runs in parallel with academic studies, with professional internships where employer placements are available.

What You Get

## What Your Students Get
- ### Certification Exam Preparation and Mentoring

Industry certification exam preparation by track, followed by three months of milestone-based mentoring toward a first client or a first job.
- ### Skills Employers Ask For

Tracks are set from employer demand in an AI-ready curriculum, built around the skills employers say they cannot find.
- ### Placement Evidence You Can Report

Follow-up surveys, monthly reports and documentary evidence where available, as agreed in the program scope, give your institution outcome data it can report.

Questions, Answered

## Questions Universities Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

We test local employer demand for your graduates’ skills and your campus labs first, and choose each country’s tracks with local partners. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, with program results from Kosovo and delivery experience in Albania.

### What Happens After the First Cohort?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays on your campus: certified local facilitators, localized curricula and campus labs that serve as blended-learning centers. Completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a partnership includes.
