# The Future: Building a Digital Factory for Bangladesh

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published July 17, 2026; updated August 1, 2026.

[image: Speakers seated at a table under a rural economic growth event banner, with a man speaking into a microphone]

Looking ahead, Chairman and Co-founder Aziz Ahmad shared a bold vision for Bangladesh’s future, one where AI, digital technologies, and human capital become the nation’s greatest competitive advantage. He believes Bangladesh’s most valuable natural resource is not beneath the ground, but in the minds of its young people.

His vision is to make Bangladesh a digital factory by embedding ICT, AI, and digital skills across every district, town, and village, enabling millions of young Bangladeshis to participate in the global knowledge economy from wherever they live. Rather than leaving their communities in search of opportunity, they can build world-class careers, serve international clients, create innovative businesses, and contribute to the nation’s prosperity.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uRsp5R3K4cY

As Mr. Ahmad often says, “Learn a skill, the world is yours.” This philosophy is more than a slogan, it is a roadmap for transforming Bangladesh into a knowledge based economy driven by innovation, entrepreneurship, and globally competitive talent.

He envisions a Bangladesh where every young person with determination and the right skills can compete on the world stage, making the country a global hub for digital talent, AI innovation, and technology-enabled services.
