# Hour of Code in Somaliland

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published December 21, 2015.

Added in October 2026 from the dated sources linked in the text.

[image: About 30 men and women, many in white caps, pose in two rows outdoors in front of a Tima-ade University banner]

In late 2015, CodersTrust’s work in Somaliland included an Hour of Code event for students, and an ICT program at Tima-ade University. Hour of Code is a worldwide one-hour introduction to coding.

That month CodersTrust invited people in Bangladesh, India, Kenya and Somaliland to take part in the Hour of Code, as its post of December 21, 2015 reported. The photograph shows a group in front of a Tima-ade University banner.

A Somali television news item of November 29, 2015, from Gabiley, names Coders Trust, the university (spelled “Timacade”) and its ICT program.
