Our Approach

We combine training, mentoring and placement support in existing labs and colleges. Your agency receives monthly reports on progress toward paid work, as agreed in the program scope.

Questions, Answered

## Questions Ministries Ask

### How Fast Can We Start?

Public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline, so the first step is a briefing for your ministry.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

We scale in phases. Her Power, a national program, met its full target with CodersTrust’s curriculum; the contract table above gives the figures. A 100,000+ program is a design option we have not yet delivered.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

We test local employer demand, lab access and facilitator supply first and choose each country’s tracks with local partners. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, with program results from Kosovo and delivery experience in Albania.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the briefing. See what a partnership includes.
