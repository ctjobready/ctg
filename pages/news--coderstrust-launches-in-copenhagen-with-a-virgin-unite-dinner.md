# CodersTrust Launches in Copenhagen with a Virgin Unite Dinner

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published April 8, 2014.

Added in October 2026 from the dated sources linked in the text.

[image: A speaker in a dark jacket holds a microphone and addresses seated dinner guests in a hotel ballroom]

CodersTrust launched in Copenhagen, Denmark, in April 2014, at a Virgin Unite dinner held in the presence of Sir Richard Branson and early Skype investor Morten Lund.

The dinner took place on April 8, 2014, in the palm court of the Hotel d’Angleterre, with talks on entrepreneurship, CodersTrust’s vision and a question-and-answer session. All guests were then invited to a party at CodersTrust’s Copenhagen offices. The Danish business daily Børsen wrote about the evening that day, and CodersTrust’s own event page describes it.

[image: Two men sit on a sofa on a stage, smiling, one holding a microphone]

That month, CodersTrust signed an agreement in Copenhagen with Grameen Solutions Ltd to train IT freelancers in Bangladesh, as The Daily Star reported on May 4, 2014.
