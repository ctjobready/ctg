## Four Jobs, One Platform

JobReady is built around four jobs. It is designed to train youth through courses that prepare for industry certification, connect learners to local, remote and global work, supply employers with job-ready talent, and give institutions a digital delivery platform. The table below says which capabilities are live today.

## Rollout Status

Categories of capability and their status, as of this page’s last update.
| Job | Capability | Status | Note |
| Train | Course storefront at jobready.global | Operating | CodersTrust’s course storefront operates at jobready.global. |
| Connect to Work | Gig-work (Earn) marketplace | Pre-Launch | In pre-launch. Until it launches, placement support is provided by CodersTrust’s placement team, and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr are also used. |
| Supply Talent | Employer marketplace | Pre-Launch | In pre-launch. Until it launches, employers are introduced to candidates through CodersTrust’s placement team. |

What Is Live Today

Partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat are live, and the course storefront operates. The employer and gig-work marketplace is in pre-launch, and the adaptive learning below is planned. AI chat is live today. Adaptive learning remains on the roadmap.

## Planned Adaptive Learning

JobReady is being built as an AI-native platform, with TalentLEAP as the basis for AI-driven personalized and adaptive learning. This is roadmap, described as what the platform is designed to do, and it is not live.

### Planned Personalized and Adaptive Learning

The platform is designed to personalize each learner’s path and to adapt learning as each learner moves through TalentLEAP’s competency levels.
