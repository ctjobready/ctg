# JobReady: CodersTrust’s Digital Education and Employment Platform

JobReady is CodersTrust’s digital education and employment platform, being built as an AI-native platform. Modules are rolling out in stages.

Last updated October 8, 2026

### On This Page
- Four Jobs
- Rollout Status

## Four Jobs, One Platform

JobReady is built around four jobs. The table below says which capabilities are live today.

### Train

Trains youth through courses that prepare for industry certification; the course storefront operates at jobready.global.

### Connect to Work

Designed to connect learners to local, remote and global work.

### Supply Talent

Designed to supply employers with job-ready talent.

### Equip Institutions

Gives institutions a digital delivery platform.

## AI-Native Roadmap: What the Platform Is Designed to Do

JobReady is being built as an AI-native platform, with TalentLEAP as the basis for AI-driven personalized and adaptive learning. Everything below is roadmap, described as what the platform is designed to do, and none of it is presented as live.

### Planned Personalized and Adaptive Learning

The platform is designed to personalize each learner’s path and to adapt learning as each learner moves through TalentLEAP’s competency levels.

## Questions About the Platform

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer and gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.
