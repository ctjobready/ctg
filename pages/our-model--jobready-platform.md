## Four Jobs, One Platform

JobReady is designed to do four jobs: train youth through courses that prepare for industry certification, connect learners to local, remote and global work, supply employers with job-ready talent, and give institutions a digital delivery platform. The table below says which capabilities are live today.

## Rollout Status

Categories of capability and their status, as of this page’s last update.
| Job | Capability | Status | Note |
| Train | Course storefront at jobready.global | Live | CodersTrust’s course storefront operates at jobready.global. |
| Connect to Work | Gig-work (Earn) marketplace | Pre-Launch | In pre-launch. Until then, placement support comes from CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr. |
| Supply Talent | Employer marketplace | Pre-Launch | In pre-launch. Until then, employers are introduced to candidates through CodersTrust’s placement team. |
| Equip Institutions | Partner portals | Live | Available to partner institutions for digital delivery. |
| Across the Platform | Marketing automation, customer relationship management and AI chat | Live | Live as part of the staged rollout. |
