Context

## Why This Program

In 2017 the World Bank Group sought market-oriented skills to help young women in Kosovo find meaningful employment.

The Women in Online Work (WoW) pilot prepared young women in Kosovo, around the country, for online work.

## Challenges
- Limited infrastructure.
- Few child- and elder-care options, leaving little time to learn.
- Cultural attitudes that discourage women from working.

## What CodersTrust Did
- Two-phase delivery that built digital literacy first.
- Interactive, collaborative sessions to keep learners engaged.
- Soft skills paired with career counseling.
- Marketplace integration: bidding on and doing real online work.

Results

## What Happened

Program Results

Program Record

In Work

102 of 150 women in work (68%)

Online Earnings

78 program completers earned a combined total of about $25,000 online

Women in Online Work was a pilot with the World Bank Group in 2016–2017. The figures above are from CodersTrust program records.

The World Bank reports that 77 trainees in the Women in Online Work pilot in Kosovo won at least one online work contract in the pilot’s first two phases.
