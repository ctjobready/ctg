# 1,000 People to Be Trained on High Demand IT Skills | Sheikh Kamal ICT Incubator Training

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published January 5, 2023; updated October 29, 2026.

[image: Architect’s rendering of the Sheikh Kamal IT Center, a curved glass building with palm trees in front]

[image: Officials signing an agreement at a table with a group standing behind them]

## Objective

Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority is setting up Sheikh Kamal IT Training and Incubation Centers at eight locations across Bangladesh, with an aim to develop tech-skilled manpower to work in the IT industry on a large scale.

## Scope

CodersTrust will provide training to 1,000 people on high demand IT skills
