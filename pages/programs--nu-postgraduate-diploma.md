## Before You Apply

Program details as stated for the National University Postgraduate Diploma.

Eligibility

Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25.

Duration

1 Year (2 Semesters).

Credit Hours and Subjects (Each Course)

40 Credit Hours and 10 Subjects.

Schedule and Intake

Admissions confirms intake dates and schedule with each applicant.

Delivery

On-Campus and Instructor-Led; online delivery is not available.

Teaching Location

Admissions confirms the teaching location with each applicant.

Program Fee

BDT 50,000.

Payment

Pay instantly online, transfer via mobile merchant accounts, visit CodersTrust Bangladesh (CTBD) branches for cash or POS payments, or make a bank deposit.

### Is the NU Postgraduate Diploma Right for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. It is right for you if you meet that and want a digital career. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
