# Privacy Policy

This website is a static site. It sets no analytics cookies and loads no third-party trackers on its own; a video loads from YouTube only after you click play, and YouTube may then set its own cookies. This page names the services involved in handling your data, and when.

## In Short
- We run no analytics and set no analytics cookies. We use no advertising or third-party tracking scripts.
- The site has no forms. Inquiries are emails that you send yourself.
- The only thing the site stores in your browser is a note that you dismissed the announcement bar.
- Services run by other organizations (the host, Google Forms, YouTube, social networks, maps) come into play only in the situations listed below.

## Every Service Involved
| Service | When It Is Involved | What It Can See |
| GitHub Pages (Hosting) | Every page view | Your IP address and request details in server logs |
| Your Email Provider | When you send us an email | Your message and address |
| Google Forms (Application Forms) | Only if you open the National University diploma application form | What you type into the form |
| jobready.global | Only if you follow a link to it | Whatever that site collects once you are on it |
| YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) | Only after you click play on a video | Your IP address, and what YouTube sets once the video loads |
| LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Google Maps | Only if you click a share, social or map link | What those services collect once you are on them |
| Your Browser (Local Storage) | If you dismiss the announcement bar | Stays on your device; we never receive it |

## Hosting and Server Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, the host’s servers record standard log data, which includes your IP address, the page requested and your browser type. We do not add analytics, and we do not use this data to identify visitors. Read GitHub’s privacy statement

(opens docs.github.com)

for how the host handles logs.

## Email Inquiries

Buttons that say "Book a Discovery Session", "Ask About Due Diligence" and similar open your own email app with a prefilled message. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in your email app. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use it to answer you and to follow up on your inquiry. Your email provider handles the message on its way.

## National University Applications

Applications for the National University postgraduate diploma use an existing Google Form. When you open it you leave this site and go to a page run by Google (application form

(opens docs.google.com)

). What you enter goes to Google Forms and to the admissions team, and Google’s own privacy policy applies to it.

## Links to Other Sites

Course catalog links go to jobready.global

(opens jobready.global)

, a separate site with its own privacy policy. Share buttons on news articles, social links and "Open in Maps" links are plain links: nothing is sent to LinkedIn, X, Facebook or Google until you click one, and then you are on their site.

## Video

Videos appear as a still image with a play button. No request goes to YouTube until you click play. After the click, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain, and YouTube sees your IP address and may store its own data on your device. If you never click play, YouTube never hears from you.

## Your Browser Storage

If you close the announcement bar at the top of the page, the site saves a small note in your browser’s local storage (the key is ct-promo-v2) so that the bar stays closed on your next visit. It is a functional setting, it stays on your device, and we never receive it. That is why we do not ask for consent for it. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

We set no cookies of our own.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to our use of it, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise these rights, email contact@coderstrust.global.

## Children

This website is written for partners, funders, employers, universities and learners. The site itself runs no forms or analytics; the hosting provider logs IP addresses. Each CodersTrust program specifies its own safeguarding and data arrangements, including those for participants under 18, which are agreed before enrollment. See governance and accountability.
