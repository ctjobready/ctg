Context

## Why This Program

In September 2021, the World Bank launched Digital Jobs Albania to help women in Albania gain better access to online work and connect with the global economy.

The initiative was implemented with the Government of Albania, CodersTrust and EuroPartners Development, a local consulting company.

## Challenges
- Women’s labor force participation in Albania is lower than men’s.
- Online freelancing takes technical skills, and the soft skills to compete for project work.

## What CodersTrust Did

The World Bank names CodersTrust as the international digital skills training provider in the partnership. The initiative gave participants:
- An intensive three-month online training program in digital skills.
- Tracks in graphic design, web development and digital marketing.
- Mentorship, and help with the soft skills needed to compete for project work on online freelancer websites.

Results

## What Happened

What the World Bank Reported

Interest

5,000+ women expressed interest, far exceeding the available spots.

Enrolled

500+ students enrolled in the three-month course.

The World Bank published these figures in May 2022, while the training was still running. They show interest and enrollment, not completion or employment.
