## What Is JobReady@Campus?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

JobReady@Campus: industry-oriented skills training in parallel with academic studies — on-campus labs used as blended-learning centers, faculty members as coordinators, preparation for industry-recognized certifications plus professional internships where employer placements are available, designed to lead to freelance work and local, remote and overseas jobs.

How It Works

## Skills Training That Runs Beside the Academic Program

Where We Stand

For universities and colleges who need their degree holders to leave with a practical route into digital work, CodersTrust offers JobReady@Campus, training with industry-certification preparation that runs in parallel with academic studies. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the whole path to earnings, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.
- When

### In Parallel with Studies

Industry-oriented skills training runs alongside the academic program.
- Where

### On-Campus Labs

Labs serve as blended-learning centers, with faculty members as coordinators.
- What

### Certification and Internships

Preparation for industry-recognized certifications, plus professional internships where employer placements are available.
- After

### Freelance, Local, Remote and Overseas Work

Mentoring and placement support lead into work.

### What a Campus Program Includes
- 100 training hours per certification (50 h lectures + 50 h hands-on labs) over 3–6 months, then three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client), then the alumni network.
- Certifications by track: Meta Blueprint / Google Ads (digital marketing), Adobe Certified Professional (design, video), QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor (digital accounting), PCEP (Python), CWP (web).

CodersTrust is not affiliated with or endorsed by these certifying bodies; it prepares learners for their exams.
- Up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel from about month 5; classes of 20 (labs) and 40 (lectures); hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators for larger programs.

## Questions Institutions Ask

### Is JobReady@Campus Right for Our Institution?

Right for you if you want employer-demand tracks with certification preparation alongside degrees. Not right if you want to replace the degree.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### Who Are Your Typical Learners?

Mostly university graduates from across Bangladesh, some of them already working before training. Among surveyed completers, 84.6% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher, 68.7% were aged 15–35 and 62.9% live outside Dhaka, across 58 of the country’s 64 districts; 47.4% were working before training.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence.

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.
