What You Get

## Built Around What Institutions Ask For
- ### Certification Exam Preparation and Mentoring

Industry certification exam preparation by track, followed by three months of milestone-based mentoring toward a first client or a first job.
- ### Skills Employers Ask For

Tracks are set from employer demand in an AI-ready curriculum, built around the skills employers say they cannot find.
- ### Placement Evidence You Can Report

Follow-up surveys, monthly reports and documentary evidence where available, as agreed in the program scope, give your institution outcome data it can report.

Questions, Answered

## Questions Institutions Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Your institution receives monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Who Is JobReady@Campus For?

Students and degree holders at universities and colleges. Across CodersTrust’s wider programs, 84.6% of surveyed completers hold a bachelor’s degree or higher.
