## Training Beside the Degree, in Your Own Labs

Our Approach

For universities and colleges who need their degree holders to leave with a practical route into digital work, CodersTrust offers JobReady@Campus, training with industry-certification preparation that runs in parallel with academic studies. We combine training, mentoring and placement support in existing labs and colleges. Partners receive monthly reports on progress toward paid work.

## Questions Institutions Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Your institution receives monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Who Are Your Typical Learners?

Mostly university graduates from across Bangladesh, some of them already working before training. Across CodersTrust’s wider programs, among surveyed completers, 84.6% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher.
