## What Is JobReady@Campus?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

JobReady@Campus: industry-oriented skills training in parallel with academic studies — on-campus labs used as blended-learning centers, faculty members as coordinators, preparation for industry-recognized certifications plus professional internships where employer placements are available, designed to lead to freelance work and local, remote and overseas jobs.

How It Works

## How the Campus Model Works

Our Approach

For universities and colleges who need their degree holders to leave with a practical route into digital work, CodersTrust offers JobReady@Campus, training with industry-certification preparation that runs in parallel with academic studies. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the path from training toward paid work, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.

### What a Campus Program Includes
- Certifications by track: Meta Blueprint / Google Ads (digital marketing), Adobe Certified Professional (design, video), QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor (digital accounting), PCEP (Python), CWP (web).

CodersTrust is not affiliated with or endorsed by these certifying bodies; it prepares learners for their exams.
- Up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel from about month 5; classes of 20 (labs) and 40 (lectures); hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators for larger programs.

## Questions Institutions Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Your institution receives monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Who Are Your Typical Learners?

Mostly university graduates from across Bangladesh, some of them already working before training. Across CodersTrust’s wider programs, among surveyed completers, 84.6% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher, 68.7% were aged 15–35 and 62.9% live outside Dhaka, across 58 of the country’s 64 districts; 47.4% were working before training.

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions: outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.
