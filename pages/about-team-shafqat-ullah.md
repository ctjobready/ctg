Shafqat Ullah is the Chief Technology Officer of CodersTrust. He is a seasoned software architect with 25+ years of experience across diverse domains, having architected award-winning enterprise products and country-scale IT solutions.

As a researcher and author, he drives foundational work in software engineering, cloud, and edge computing. He serves as an expert advisor to global initiatives including Fortune 250 companies, Silicon Valley VCs, the UN, and the Government of Bangladesh.

An Affiliate Member of Intel’s Neuromorphic AI Research program, he graduated in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT). His expertise spans AI and cloud-native SaaS and mobile app design and architecture, and large-scale tech skills training since 2002.
