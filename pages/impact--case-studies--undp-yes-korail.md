Context

## Why This Program

Young people in Dhaka’s urban informal settlements face limited access to education and training, discrimination, and little exposure to careers or networks.

In 2017 CodersTrust trained young people from the Korail settlement in Dhaka, with UNDP, the Government of Bangladesh and Swanirvar Bangladesh. Program completers went on to mentor local youth.

## Challenges
- Day labor pulled young people away from training.
- Limited access to electricity, computers and money to study.
- No computer literacy and too little English to work with clients.

## What CodersTrust Did
- An awareness campaign, courtyard meetings and parent counseling in Korail.
- A fully equipped lab to practice and work.
- A basic English communication module.
- One-to-one mentoring for students.

## What Happened

Program Results, 2017

Program Record

UNDP YES (Youth Employment through Skills), Korail settlement, Dhaka, with UNDP, the Government of Bangladesh and Swanirvar Bangladesh, 2017: 350 trained; 97.5% completed the program; 67% of the 350 trained found their first job within three months.
