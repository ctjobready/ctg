# Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to read this site and use it, with a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification or a phone. We build to WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## Our Target

The target for this website is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. That includes the newer success criteria, such as focus not being hidden behind sticky page elements (2.4.11) and large enough touch targets (2.5.8). This statement describes the target and the measures we take. It is not a certificate of conformance, and we keep working on gaps.

## What We Have Done
- Structure. Pages are built with one main heading, a logical heading order and landmarks, and a “Skip to Main Content” link comes first.
- Keyboard. Menus, the mobile drawer, accordions, carousels and filters work with a keyboard. Focus is visible, and the sticky header and announcement bar are designed not to cover the element that has focus.
- Color and type. Text and controls use a color theme designed to meet AA contrast. Pages are designed to reflow to a 320 px width without sideways scrolling, and text can be enlarged.
- Motion. Animations stop for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion, and the final values of counters are in the page without animation. The logo marquee has a pause button.
- Works without JavaScript. Core content, statistics and links are in the page HTML, so they remain available when scripts do not run.
- Images, charts and maps. Images have alternative text or are marked decorative; photos in older news posts are an exception (see Known Limitations). Charts have a data table, and the map has a location table (below).
- Testing. Pages are checked with automated accessibility tests at phone and desktop widths.

## The World Map

The map on the global reach page is a supplementary visual. Its pins are hidden from assistive technology and are not focus targets. Everything a pin shows is also in the location table on the same page, which is always visible and can be read and navigated by keyboard and screen reader. The table carries every detail the map shows.

## Known Limitations
- Older news posts. News articles are carried over from our earlier website and lightly edited. Some photos in them have generic or empty alternative text, and some posts link to long web addresses. We are improving these as we edit posts.
- Embedded and external content. Videos play on YouTube, and their captions and controls are YouTube’s. Pages we link to, such as jobready.global and the application form for the National University diploma, are run by others and may not meet the same standard.
- Press logos. Third-party logos are shown as supplied by their owners.
