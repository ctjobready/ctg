The Cost of Waiting

## Every Year of Waiting Costs a Cohort.

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT.

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Young people who wait risk falling behind as entry-level work changes.

### Climate-Exposed Coastal Districts Have Been Losing Their Young People.

Bangladesh’s 19 coastal districts hold 44.8M people (28% of the population); net out-migration of 20–24-year-olds reached 64.9% in Jhalokathi (census 2001–2011); Cyclone Amphan (2020) displaced 2.5M people.

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done: government contracts with training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff, and a national program that trained 25,125 women across 130 upazilas, for which CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs run hub-and-spoke — up to 10 cohorts in parallel, certified local facilitators and blended delivery in existing labs and colleges; in Bangladesh, 9,000+ government digital labs could host blended learning; lab access and seats are confirmed per program. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we plan phase by phase; we have not yet delivered one at that size.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are exposed, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### How Fast Can We Start?

For grant-funded pilots, about three weeks from first conversation to agreement, with the first cohort in training 6–8 weeks after signing. Week 1 is a discovery session, week 2 co-design and week 3 the agreement; public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.
