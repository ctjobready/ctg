Questions, Answered

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand in your portfolio countries, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh; the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it, and Digital Jobs Albania adds delivery experience; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### Can You Reach Women and Other Priority Groups?

Our women-focused programs report work outcomes; see Proof in Practice above. For other priority groups, we measure outcomes in each pilot. The randomized trial studied women aged 18–35 in Dhaka with at least a Secondary School Certificate (SSC), so it does not speak to other groups.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a partnership includes.
