The Cost of Waiting

## Training Without Placement Spends Budget Without Earnings

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT’s Release

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Training has to follow current employer demand, not last year’s job market.

### A Review of Nine Trials Found Only Three with Significant Employment Impacts

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Placement and tracking have to be built into the program, not added afterward.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

## An Independent Randomized Trial

For every 100 women offered a place, about 10 more were in work at follow-up than among 100 comparable women in the control group (+10.3 percentage points).

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

Objections, Answered

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand in your portfolio countries, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### Can It Reach Women and Marginalized Groups?

For women, yes: women-focused programs report their own results (WSDFM 71% in work; Women in Online Work, Kosovo 68%; Her Power 372 of 800 tracked women in work, 47%, tracked from the 2,500 trained directly by CodersTrust). For other priority groups, we have early delivery experience and measure outcomes in each pilot; the randomized trial studied women aged 18–35 in Dhaka with at least a Secondary School Certificate (SSC), so it does not speak to the other groups.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing as further levels are published.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.
