## Questions About This Course

### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

After this course you can describe and show the work you did in modules such as WordPress Design, Content Marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Web & Google Analytics, and in the final module of internship, project work and a seminar. What you do next depends on your own experience, portfolio and the local market.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. It is right for you if you meet that and want a digital career. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
