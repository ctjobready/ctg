## Questions About This Course

### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

Build practical work in WordPress design, content marketing, SEO and analytics, followed by the final internship, project work and seminar module. Your next step depends on your experience, portfolio and local market.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. Choose Digital Marketing if you want to study SEO, social media, advertising and analytics. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
