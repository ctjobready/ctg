## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

Admissions

## Before You Apply

Program details as stated for this course in the National University Postgraduate Diploma.

Eligibility

Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25.

Duration

12 Months, 40 Credit Hours and 10 Subjects.

Schedule and Intake

Admissions confirms intake dates and schedule with each applicant.

Delivery

On-Campus and Instructor-Led; online delivery is not available.

Teaching Location

Admissions confirms the teaching location with each applicant.

Program Fee

BDT 50,000.

Payment

Pay instantly online, transfer via mobile merchant accounts, visit CTBD branches for cash or POS payments, or make a bank deposit.

Recognition

CodersTrust is an affiliated college (College Code: 6634) under the National University of Bangladesh.

How to Apply

Complete the application form. For help, Call +880 1958‑220802 or email hello@coderstrustbd.com.

## Questions About This Course

### What Will I Study?

10 modules: Fundamentals of Digital Marketing; WordPress Design (Practical); AdWords & PPC; Content Marketing (Practical); Affiliate & E-mail Marketing (Practical); E-Commerce; Social Media Marketing; Search Engine Optimization (SEO) - Practical; Web & Google Analytics (Practical); and a final module of internship, project work and seminar.

### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

You will have completed the 10 modules listed above, including a final module of internship, project work and a seminar. What you can do next depends on your own experience, portfolio and the local market.

### Where Do I Find Fees, Schedule and Eligibility?

The fee, duration, eligibility and recognition details are in Before You Apply above. Admissions confirms intake dates and schedule with each applicant. Admissions confirms the teaching location with each applicant. To ask, Call +880 1958‑220802.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Right for you if you hold a degree and want a digital career. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
