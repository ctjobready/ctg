## How People Are Protected

Each program design specifies consent, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response, referral pathways and arrangements for participants under 18. These are agreed and documented before enrollment.

## Independent Evaluation and Funder Reporting

Independent evaluation is welcome. A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed.

As agreed in the program scope, funders receive monthly reports from the first cohort.

## Common Due-Diligence Questions

### How Do You Track and Verify Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Where available, offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts document work and earnings.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.
