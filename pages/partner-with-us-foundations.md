## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

The Cost of Waiting

## Every Year of Delay Is a Cohort Without a Path.

### AI Is Reshaping Entry Work.

Globally, AI and information processing are expected to create 11M jobs and displace 9M by 2030; across all trends 170M created vs 92M displaced.

What It Means Programs have to track where entry-level work is going.

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT’s Release.

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means Tracks chosen from current employer demand keep pace.

An estimated 62 million young people aged 15–24 with higher-secondary education or more (HSC+) (range 61–78 million) are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, MENA and Sub-Saharan Africa.

What It Means Every year of delay is a cohort without a path.

## Questions Funders Ask

Right for you if you want to fund outcomes you can see. Not right if you want a one-off donation with no reporting.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done: government contracts with training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff, and the curriculum for a national program that trained 25,125 women (with 2,500 women trained directly); separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs run hub-and-spoke — up to 10 cohorts in parallel, certified local facilitators and blended delivery in existing labs and colleges; in Bangladesh, 9,000+ government digital labs could host blended learning; lab access and seats are confirmed per program. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we plan phase by phase; we have not yet delivered one at that size.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Evidence includes payslips and offer letters for jobs, platform logs or bank receipts for freelancing; requirements are agreed in co-design. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Is There Independent Evidence?

Yes — a randomized controlled trial by BIGD (BRAC University) of CodersTrust’s WSDFM program studied women in Dhaka trained in 2020, on a 200-hour predecessor of today’s 100-hour track. Women offered a place were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed (52.8% of the control group was employed), and their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher. For women who attended, income was 54% higher and employment 26% higher. The trial surveyed 599 women at follow-up (report published May 2022). For context, a review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment, and CodersTrust contracts through CT USA, Inc. in the United States or CodersTrust Bangladesh in Bangladesh, with program-specific due-diligence documents confirmed with funders during co-design. Each program design covers accessible selection, consent-based data collection, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response and referral pathways, with guardian consent and supervision for any participant under 18; funders receive privacy-appropriate data.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

What the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP organizes skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence.

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.

- Pilot First Start with one cohort and see its results before you commit further.
- Milestone Reporting Monthly reports and a quarterly steering review show progress against the scorecard.
- Expansion Is the Funder’s Decision You choose whether and how far to scale.
- Independent Evaluation An independent evaluation can be budgeted separately where you want one.
