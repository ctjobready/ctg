# Impact and Evidence

The randomized trial shows cause for one predecessor program; the 2026 survey describes what surveyed completers report; program records show results for named programs.

[image: A group of women in matching purple at a Her Power program event]

In Short

A randomized trial of CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program found that women offered a place were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Among surveyed completers, employment was 47.4% before training and 77.9% at the October 2026 survey.

## The Evidence Ladder: Four Kinds of Evidence

Strongest for cause first. Each rung says what it shows and what it does not show.
