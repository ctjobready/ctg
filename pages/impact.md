# Impact You Can Verify

Program records from Kosovo and Bangladesh, an independent trial in Dhaka and a 2026 survey of completers. Each figure names its kind of evidence.

[image: Women learners at computers in a training room in Kosovo, one in the foreground turned toward the front of the room]

In Short

A randomized trial of CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program found that women offered a place were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Among surveyed completers, employment was 47.4% before training and 77.9% at the October 2026 survey.

How to Read the Evidence

## Four Kinds of Evidence, Strongest First

Each rung says what it shows and what it does not show.
| Kind of Evidence | What It Shows | What It Does Not Show |
| Independent Evaluation (BIGD) Randomized Trial | The effect of one CodersTrust program (WSDFM) on women in Dhaka, evaluated by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development against a randomly chosen control group. | Results for other countries, groups or today’s standard track. |
| Impact Survey 2026 Surveyed Completers | What alumni who responded report about work and income after training. | Cause: respondents are not a random sample and may over-represent successful learners. |
| Program Results Program Record | What each named program recorded (in work, or a first job within three months), from CodersTrust program records. | What would have happened without training. |
| Learner Stories Individual Story | What the path can look like for one person. | A typical or promised outcome; some are outliers and say so. |
