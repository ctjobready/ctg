## What Is YouthWIDE?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

YouthWIDE (Youth Workforce Inclusion for Digital Economy) is CodersTrust’s youth digital-employment program. In Bangladesh it is a national initiative that aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth for digital employment by 2030, a national ambition that depends on government and donor commitments, not a delivered figure. In other countries it runs as co-funded country cohorts, designed for South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa; current outcome evidence comes from Bangladesh and Kosovo.

Each trainee gets 100 training hours per certification and three months of milestone-based mentoring, with placement support and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course.

## Training Without Work Entry Spends the Budget Without the Result.

### A Review of Nine Trials Found Only Three with Significant Employment Impacts.

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Too many funders receive attendance and completion counts but little evidence of earnings.

### Each Cohort Is Documented from a Baseline at Enrollment, with Tracer Results at 3, 6 and 12 Months.

We document results through a baseline at enrollment and follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months; where available, offer letters, payslips and platform or bank records document work and earnings.

What It Means

Without that record, an agency cannot see who entered work or began earning after training.

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT’s Release.

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Tracks have to follow current employer demand, not last year’s job market.

### 72% of Employers Worldwide Report Difficulty Finding the Talent They Need.

Globally, 72% of employers report difficulty finding the talent they need; AI skills are now the hardest to find.

What It Means

Hiring alone does not close the skills gap, so tracks need to build the skills employers ask for.

## One Delivery Model, from Selection to Paid Work

Where We Stand

For development partners and funders who need educated, unemployed youth in paid digital work in South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, or Sub-Saharan Africa, CodersTrust offers YouthWIDE, the program through which funders deploy its integrated training-to-earnings model in a country. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the whole path to earnings, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.

### The Delivery Model in Detail
- #### Training and Mentoring

100 training hours per certification (50 h lectures + 50 h hands-on labs) over 3–6 months, then three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client), then the alumni network.
- #### Certifications by Track

Meta Blueprint / Google Ads (digital marketing), Adobe Certified Professional (design, video), QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor (digital accounting), PCEP (Python), CWP (web).

CodersTrust is not affiliated with or endorsed by these certifying bodies; it prepares learners for their exams.
- #### Cohorts in Parallel

Up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel from about month 5; classes of 20 (labs) and 40 (lectures); hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators for larger programs.
- #### Connectivity-Light Delivery

Runs on 1.5 Mbps; 720p lessons downloadable for offline viewing.
- #### Accessible Readiness Assessment

A readiness assessment, written test and interview guide selection and placement into a career or foundation track; a small number of applicants may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.
- #### Foundation Tracks

Learners with limited computer or English skills get extra foundation hours and facilitators (scoped in co-design), and their outcomes are reported by track.
- #### Placement Support

Assessment-based career advising, CV writing, job-application and interview preparation, industry internships and apprenticeships, job fairs, employer matching and freelance-marketplace onboarding.
- #### Quality Assurance

Common practical-assessment rubrics, sampled moderation of portfolios and assessments, trainer observation and remediation before the next cohort; completion, competency and external-exam results reported separately.

Objections, Answered

## Questions Funders and Agencies Ask

### Is YouthWIDE Right for Our Agency?

Right for you if you want employment outcomes tracked after blended training delivered through existing government labs and colleges. Not right if you need a single-vendor 100,000+ rollout in one phase: we plan the National option phase by phase.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done. Six government contracts have training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff. Her Power, a national program of DoICT, the ICT Division, trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas); CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly. Separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs run hub-and-spoke: up to 10 cohorts in parallel, with certified local facilitators and blended delivery in existing labs and colleges. In Bangladesh, 9,000+ government digital labs could host blended learning; lab access and seats are confirmed per program. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we plan phase by phase; we have not yet delivered one at that size.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Planned for every cohort, as agreed in the program scope: a baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings is collected where available: offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings. Monthly reports cover enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### How Fast Can We Start?

For grant-funded pilots, about three weeks from first conversation to agreement, with the first cohort in training 6–8 weeks after signing. Week 1 is a discovery session, week 2 co-design and week 3 the agreement; public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### How Is CodersTrust Different from an Online Course Platform?

Self-paced course platforms focus on content access; our programs add mentoring, certification exam preparation and placement support, agreed in each program’s scope. Every TalentLEAP cohort pairs 100 training hours per certification with three months of milestone-based mentoring, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), placement support and outcome tracking for 12 months.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it. We set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis. CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories.

### Can It Reach Women and Marginalized Groups?

For women, yes; for the most excluded groups we have early experience, and we measure outcomes in each pilot. Women-focused programs report their own results: WSDFM 71% in work; Women in Online Work, Kosovo 68%; Her Power 372 of 800 tracked women, from the 2,500 trained directly by CodersTrust, in work (47%). Our first UNDP-supported pilot (2015) trained youth in Dhaka’s Korail settlement, and we are planning a program with the Bangladesh Computer Council and Bandhu Welfare Society to train 5,000 third-gender people. YouthWIDE in Bangladesh aims to reach madrasah youth, climate-vulnerable coastal youth and indigenous communities, with women a priority in every group. The randomized trial studied women aged 18–35 in Dhaka with at least a Secondary School Certificate (SSC), so it does not speak to the other groups.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.

Accountability

Every pilot is judged on cost per completer and per verified outcome, with thresholds agreed with you before the first cohort starts.

Pricing

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions: outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course. Devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

Scale

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done: government contracts with training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff, and a national program that trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas), for which CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs use existing labs and colleges, certified local facilitators and blended delivery, with up to 10 cohorts running in parallel.

Services Behind a Phase
- CodersTrust’s role in Her Power: curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines; Training of Trainers; 2,500 women trained directly; 54,609 manuals and guidelines distributed.
- Contract scope: LMS and operation training for 3,120+ staff, for the Digital Lab Establishment Project (Phase II).

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence.

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.

A pilot comes first: prove the model in your labs before you commit to a wider phase. Thresholds are agreed with you before the first cohort starts, and there is a review gate before each phase.

### What You Decide at Each Review
- Review the Reports Together. You and CodersTrust hold a quarterly steering review of the monthly reports.
- Agree a Corrective Plan. A missed scorecard threshold triggers a corrective plan, agreed with you.
- Decide on Expansion. Expansion is the funder’s decision, taken on the scorecard results.
- Commission an Independent Evaluation. Independent evaluation is welcome, and we support it.
