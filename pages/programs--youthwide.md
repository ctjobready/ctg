Decision Summary

## YouthWIDE at a Glance

Designed for Three Regions

South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa, as co-funded country cohorts. Bangladesh is where most delivery has been, with an initiative of its own that aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth by 2030, depending on government and donor commitments.

Who It Is For

Governments, development partners and funders who want to report on educated, unemployed and underemployed young people moving into paid digital work.

Delivery

Blended training in existing labs and colleges, with certified local facilitators; a central team coordinating local labs for larger programs.

Training and Mentoring

100 training hours per certification with the exam fee included, then three months of milestone-based mentoring.

Placement Support

Placement support into local, remote and global work.

Reporting

Monthly reports and follow-up surveys, with work and earnings checked against offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts where available, as agreed in the program scope.

- Trained Youth Who Never Reach Paid Work

Questions, Answered

## Questions Funders and Agencies Ask

### Is YouthWIDE Right for Our Organization?

Right for you if you want employment outcomes tracked after blended training delivered through existing labs and colleges. YouthWIDE is not the right fit if you need a 100,000+ rollout delivered by one provider in a single phase; we plan national delivery phase by phase.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

We test local employer demand first and choose each country’s tracks with local partners. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, with program results from Kosovo and delivery experience in Albania.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

Her Power, a national program of DoICT, the ICT Division, trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas), meeting its target. CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,400 women directly.

Larger programs are designed to run in phases, up to 10 cohorts in parallel, with certified local facilitators. A 100,000+ phase is a design option; we have not yet delivered a program at that size.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

As agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports for every cohort, with documentary evidence of earnings where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### How Fast Can We Start?

Target timing for grant-funded pilots: about three weeks from the first conversation to agreement, then 6–8 weeks from signing to training. Public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.
