## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

The Challenge

## Education Alone Is Not Leading to Jobs

Millions of educated young people are out of work and out of training. Your training budget needs to show who starts earning.

### What Gets in the Way
- Trained Youth Who Never Reach Paid Work

A course ends, and the way into a job, a freelance client or a business of their own is left to the learner.
- Results You Cannot Verify or Report

Enrollment is easy to count. Showing who started earning, to a board or an evaluator, is much harder.
- Skills That Age Fast as AI Changes Entry-Level Work

AI is shifting which entry-level skills employers ask for, so last year’s course list can miss this year’s demand.

62 million

The Cost of Waiting

## Training and Evidence Fall Behind While You Wait

### AI Is Changing Freelance Demand

On one major freelancing platform, automation-prone writing and coding postings fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs within eight months of ChatGPT’s release (Demirci, Hannane & Zhu, 2025).

What It Means

Training has to follow current employer demand, not last year’s job market.

### Training Does Not Always Lead to Employment

A 2017 World Bank review of nine randomized vocational-training trials found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Without outcome tracking, funders cannot tell which programs reach earnings.

## What a Supported Youth Cohort Includes
- ### Training, Mentoring and Placement Support

100 training hours per certification, followed by three months of milestone-based mentoring and placement support, as agreed in the program scope. Each track’s work-entry plan is agreed before enrollment.

- ### Tracks Chosen from Employer Demand

A curriculum designed to follow the skills employers ask for as entry-level tasks change.

Questions, Answered

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

We test local employer demand first and choose each country’s tracks with local partners. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, with program results from Kosovo and delivery experience in Albania.

### Does the Trial Apply to Your Program?

The randomized trial evaluated CodersTrust’s 200-hour WSDFM program for women in Dhaka, not today’s 100-hour tracks. Results for other programs come from the October 2026 survey of completers and from program records, which are descriptive.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

A pilot scorecard is agreed before the first cohort. As agreed in the program scope, partners receive monthly reports and follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months. Independent evaluation is welcome.

Pilot Scorecard

Five measures, agreed before the first cohort
- Completion
- Certification
- First Paid Work
- Sustained Work

For example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum.
- Cost per Completer and per Verified Outcome

### How Pricing Works

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.
