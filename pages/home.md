## For Too Many Graduates, a Degree Is Not Turning into a Job.

An estimated 62 million young people aged 15–24 with higher-secondary education or more (HSC+) (range 61–78 million) are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, MENA and Sub-Saharan Africa; in Bangladesh, graduate unemployment is 13.5% vs 2.0% for holders of the Secondary School Certificate (SSC).

The Evidence

## An Independent Randomized Trial

For every 100 women offered a place, about 10 more were in work at follow-up than among 100 comparable women in the control group (+10.3 percentage points).

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

Two kinds of evidence, kept apart: a randomized trial of a predecessor program, and a survey of completers.

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are exposed, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### How Pricing Works

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions: outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course. Devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.

### How We Scale

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done: government contracts with training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff, and a national program that trained 25,125 women across 130 upazilas, for which CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs run hub-and-spoke — up to 10 cohorts in parallel, certified local facilitators and blended delivery in existing labs and colleges.

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence.

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.
