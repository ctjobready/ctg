The Challenge

## Education Is Not Turning into Jobs

Millions of educated young people are out of work and out of training, and training budgets rarely show who starts earning.

### What Gets in the Way
- Trained youth who never reach paid work

A course ends, and the way into a job, a freelance client or a business of their own is left to the learner.
- Results you cannot verify or report

Enrollment is easy to count. Showing who started earning, to a board or an evaluator, is much harder.
- Skills that age fast as AI changes entry-level work

AI is shifting which entry-level skills employers ask for, so last year’s course list can miss this year’s demand.

The Cost of Waiting

## Tracks and Evidence Fall Behind While You Wait

### Automation-Prone Freelance Postings Fell After ChatGPT

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Tracks have to follow current employer demand, not last year’s job market.

### Only Three of Nine Training Trials Showed a Significant Employment Impact

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Without outcome tracking, funders cannot tell which programs reach earnings.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

A first cohort can show who is earning, and you decide on the evidence.

Objections, Answered

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### Does the Trial Apply to Your Program?

The randomized trial evaluated a 200-hour predecessor program for women in Dhaka, so it is evidence about that program, not a measurement of today’s 100-hour tracks. Results for later programs come from the October 2026 survey of completers and from program records, which are descriptive: they show what happened, not what would have happened without the program.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.
