The Challenge

## For Too Many Graduates, a Degree Is Not Turning into a Job.

An estimated 62 million young people aged 15–24 with higher-secondary education or more (range 61–78 million) are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa. In Bangladesh, graduate unemployment is 13.5% vs 2.0% for holders of the Secondary School Certificate (SSC).

- Trained youth who never reach paid work

- Results you cannot verify or report

- Skills that age fast as AI changes entry-level work

AI is shifting which entry-level skills employers ask for, so last year’s course list can miss this year’s demand.

The Cost of Waiting

## Every Year of Waiting Costs a Cohort.

### Automation-Prone Freelance Postings Fell After ChatGPT

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Tracks have to follow current employer demand, not last year’s job market.

### Most Employers Report Difficulty Finding Talent

Globally, 72% of employers report difficulty finding the talent they need; AI skills are now the hardest to find.

What It Means

Delaying updates to tracks and work-entry support risks preparing learners for yesterday’s demand.

### Only Three of Nine Training Trials Showed an Employment Impact

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Without outcome tracking, funders cannot tell which programs reach earnings.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

A first cohort can show who is earning, and you decide on the evidence.

Objections, Answered

## Questions Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it. We set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis. CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories.

### Does the Trial Apply to Your Program?

The randomized trial evaluated a 200-hour predecessor program for women in Dhaka. Our current tracks are 100 training hours per certification, so the trial is evidence about the predecessor program, not a measurement of today’s tracks. Results for current programs come from the October 2026 survey and program records, which are descriptive: they show what happened, not what would have happened without the program.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Planned for every cohort, as agreed in the program scope: a baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings is collected where available: offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings. Monthly reports cover enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are exposed, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### How Pricing Works

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions: outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course. Devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.

### How We Scale

We scale in phases, starting from what we have done: government contracts with training scopes ranging from 120+ professionals to 3,120+ digital-lab staff, and a national program that trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas), for which CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; separately, 10,000 teachers were trained to teach online during COVID-19. Larger programs use existing labs and colleges, certified local facilitators and blended delivery, with up to 10 cohorts running in parallel.
