Admissions

## Before You Apply

Program details as stated for this course in the National University Postgraduate Diploma.

Eligibility

To enroll in this program, students are required to have completed their honors degree or its equivalent.

Duration

12 Months, 40 Credit Hours and 10 Subjects.

Schedule and Intake

Confirmed with admissions before you apply.

Delivery

On-Campus and Instructor-Led; online delivery is not available.

Teaching Location

Confirmed with admissions before you apply.

Program Fee

BDT 50,000.

Payment

Pay instantly online, transfer via mobile merchant accounts, visit CTBD branches for cash or POS payments, or make a bank deposit.

Recognition

Postgraduate Diploma Courses Offered by National University in Affiliation with CodersTrust Bangladesh.

How to Apply

Complete the application form. For help, Call +880 1958‑220802 or email hello@coderstrustbd.com.

Who It Is For

Curriculum

## What You Study

Modules, in order. The final module combines internship, project work and a seminar; internship arrangements are confirmed with admissions.
- ### Step 1: Introduction to ICT
- ### Step 2: Office Automation
- ### Step 3: Java Programming
- ### Step 4: Database Management Systems
- ### Step 5: Multimedia Design and Developments
- ### Step 6: System Maintenance and Troubleshooting
- ### Step 7: Computer Networks Maintenance
- ### Step 8: Mobile Application Development
- ### Step 9: Web Application & Web Site Design
- ### Step 10: Internship, Project Work and Seminar

The fee, duration, eligibility and recognition details are in Before You Apply above. Intake dates and the teaching location are confirmed with admissions before you apply. To ask, Call +880 1958‑220802.
