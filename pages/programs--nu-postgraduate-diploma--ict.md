Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. Right for you if you meet that and want to add practical digital skills. If you want a short self-paced course instead, see JobReady.global.

Questions, Answered

## Questions About This Course

### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

Build practical work in programming, databases, networks and mobile applications, followed by the final internship, project work and seminar module. Your next step depends on your experience, portfolio and local market.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. Choose Information and Communication Technology (ICT) if you want to study programming, databases, networks and mobile or web development. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
