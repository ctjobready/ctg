Context

## Why This Program

In 2017 the World Bank Group sought market-oriented skills to help young women in Kosovo find meaningful employment.

The Women in Online Work (WoW) pilot tested whether online work suits young women in Kosovo around the country.

## Challenges

- Weak infrastructure in a post-war country.
- Few child- and elder-care options, leaving little time to learn.
- Cultural attitudes that discourage women from working.
- Few training materials designed for online learning.

## What CodersTrust Did

- Two-phase delivery that built digital literacy first.
- Interactive, collaborative sessions to keep learners engaged.
- Accessible, engaging digital learning materials.
- Soft skills paired with career counseling.
- Marketplace integration: bidding on and doing real online work.

Results

## What Happened

Program Results

Women in Online Work (WoW), Kosovo, with the World Bank Group, 2017: 102 of 150 women in work (68%); 78 graduates earned a combined total of about $25,000 online; 56 won online contracts (~180 jobs); 10+ founded IT ventures.

Women in Online Work was a pilot with the World Bank Group in 2017. The figures above are from CodersTrust program records.
