Who It Is For

## For Degree Holders Who Want a Digital Career

Right for you if you hold a degree and want to add practical digital skills. If you want a short self-paced course instead, see JobReady.global.

### Roles This Diploma Relates To

- IT Manager or IT Director
- Software Developer or Programmer
- Network Administrator or Network Engineer
- Database Administrator or Data Analyst
- Web Developer or Web Designer
- System Analyst or Business Analyst
- Mobile App Developer or Mobile Solutions Architect

Curriculum

## What You Study

Modules, in order. The final module combines internship, project work and a seminar; internship arrangements are confirmed with admissions.

- 1 Step 1: Introduction to ICT
- 2 Step 2: Office Automation
- 3 Step 3: Java Programming
- 4 Step 4: Database Management Systems
- 5 Step 5: Multimedia Design and Developments
- 6 Step 6: System Maintenance and Troubleshooting
- 7 Step 7: Computer Networks Maintenance
- 8 Step 8: Mobile Application Development
- 9 Step 9: Web Application & Web Site Design
- 10 Step 10: Internship, Project Work and Seminar
