## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

An estimated 62 million educated young people aged 15–24 are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa.

In a randomized trial of CodersTrust’s predecessor program, women offered a place were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed.

How It Works

## One Integrated Path, from Assessment to Career

Learn. Earn. Prosper. Each stage prepares learners for the next, and every supported cohort agrees an outcome-tracking plan before delivery begins.

For YouthWIDE and JobReady@Campus supported cohorts, this path is the delivery model. JobReady@Work, the NU Postgraduate Diploma and SuperKids have their own delivery structures.

Delivery Model

## How a Program Is Delivered

The design choices that make the path work for learners who start from different places.

### Supporting Different Starting Points

### Accessible Readiness Assessment

Tests and an interview route applicants to a career track or a foundation track. Selection uses a customized psychometric analysis, a written test and an interview; a small number may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

### Foundation Tracks

Learners with limited computer or English skills get extra foundation hours and facilitators, scoped in co-design. Their outcomes are reported by track.

### Connectivity-Light Delivery

Lessons run on a 1.5 Mbps connection, and 720p lessons can be downloaded for offline viewing.

### Delivering at Scale

### Blended Training with Certification

Each certification takes 100 training hours (50 hours of lectures and 50 hours of hands-on labs) over 3–6 months. Three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring follow, with placement support alongside. Mentoring works toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client). Learners then join the alumni network.

### Hub-and-Spoke Delivery at Scale

From about month 5, programs can run up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel, in classes of 20 (labs) and 40 (lectures). Larger programs use hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators.

### Placement Support

Placement support includes assessment-based career advising, CV writing, job-application and interview preparation, industry internships and apprenticeships, job fairs, employer matching and freelance-marketplace onboarding.

Quality

## Quality Assurance

Programs use common practical-assessment rubrics, sampled moderation of portfolios and assessments, and trainer observation, with remediation before the next cohort. Completion, competency and external-exam results are reported separately.

Safeguarding and Data

## Safeguarding and Data Protection Are Agreed per Program

Each program design specifies consent, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response, referral pathways and arrangements for participants under 18. These are agreed and documented before enrollment.

Evidence

## Evidence Behind the Approach

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

Among surveyed completers, employment rose from 47.4% before training to 77.9% at the October 2026 survey.

## Questions Partners Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings is collected where available: offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings. Monthly reports cover enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### How Fast Can We Start?

For grant-funded pilots, about three weeks from first conversation to agreement, with the first cohort in training 6–8 weeks after signing. Week 1 is a discovery session, week 2 co-design and week 3 the agreement; public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. Each program design covers accessible selection, consent-based data collection, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response and referral pathways, with guardian consent and supervision for any participant under 18; funders receive privacy-appropriate data.
