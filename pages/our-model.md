## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

How It Works

## Three Stages: Learn, Earn, Prosper

Each stage prepares learners for the next. Partners agree an outcome-tracking plan for every supported cohort before delivery begins.

For YouthWIDE and JobReady@Campus supported cohorts, this path is the delivery model. JobReady@Work, the NU Postgraduate Diploma and SuperKids have their own delivery structures.

### The TalentLEAP Stage Pipeline
- Learn
- Step 1, TalentLEAP stage Learn

#### Assess

- Step 2, TalentLEAP stage Learn

#### Learn

- Step 3, TalentLEAP stage Learn

Certification exam preparation, exam fee included.

Milestone-based mentoring and placement support toward the track’s work-entry goal.

Career paths, continuing development and the alumni network. Further TalentLEAP levels are planned.

Assess, Learn and Prepare for Certification make up the Learn stage; Earn and Prosper follow.

How TalentLEAP Works

Delivery Model

## Designed for Learners Who Start from Different Places

### Accessible Readiness Assessment

Tests and an interview route applicants to a career track or a foundation track. Some applicants may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

### Foundation Tracks

Learners with limited computer or English skills get extra foundation hours and facilitators, scoped in co-design. Their outcomes are reported by track.

### Learning with Limited Connectivity

Lessons work over a 1.5 Mbps connection, and 720p lessons can be downloaded for offline viewing.

### Blended Training with Certification

Each certification track includes 100 training hours over 3–6 months. Three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring follow, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal.

### Local Labs, Coordinated Delivery

From about month 5, programs are designed to run up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel, using existing labs and certified local facilitators.

### Placement Support

Support includes assessment-based career advising, resume writing, job-application and interview preparation, industry internships and apprenticeships, job fairs, employer matching and freelance-marketplace onboarding.

Safeguarding and Data

## Safeguarding and Data Protection Are Agreed per Program

Each program design specifies consent, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response, referral pathways and arrangements for participants under 18. These are agreed and documented before enrollment.

## Evidence Behind the Approach

A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed.

## Questions Partners Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

As agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports for every cohort, with documentary evidence of earnings where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### How Fast Can We Start?

For grant-funded pilots, allow about three weeks from the first conversation to agreement, then 6–8 weeks from signing to training. Public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a supported cohort includes.
