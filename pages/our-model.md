## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

An estimated 62 million educated young people aged 15–24 are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa.

How It Works

## Three Stages: Learn, Earn, Prosper

Each stage prepares learners for the next, and every supported cohort agrees an outcome-tracking plan before delivery begins.

For YouthWIDE and JobReady@Campus supported cohorts, this path is the delivery model. JobReady@Work, the NU Postgraduate Diploma and SuperKids have their own delivery structures.

Assess, Learn and Prepare for Certification make up the Learn stage; Earn and Prosper follow.

How TalentLEAP Works

Delivery Model

## Designed for Learners Who Start from Different Places

### Accessible Readiness Assessment

Tests and an interview route applicants to a career track or a foundation track. A small number may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

### Foundation Tracks

Learners with limited computer or English skills get extra foundation hours and facilitators, scoped in co-design. Their outcomes are reported by track.

### Connectivity-Light Delivery

Lessons run on a 1.5 Mbps connection, and 720p lessons can be downloaded for offline viewing.

### Blended Training with Certification

Each certification takes 100 training hours over 3–6 months. Three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring follow, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal.

### Hub-and-Spoke Delivery at Scale

From about month 5, programs can run up to 10 certification cohorts in parallel. Larger programs use hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators.

### Placement Support

Support includes assessment-based career advising, CV writing, job-application and interview preparation, industry internships and apprenticeships, job fairs, employer matching and freelance-marketplace onboarding.

Safeguarding and Data

## Safeguarding and Data Protection Are Agreed per Program

Each program design specifies consent, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response, referral pathways and arrangements for participants under 18. These are agreed and documented before enrollment.

Evidence

## Evidence Behind the Approach

A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

Among surveyed completers, employment was 47.4% before training and 77.9% at the October 2026 survey.

## Questions Partners Ask

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

As agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports for every cohort, with documentary evidence of earnings where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### How Fast Can We Start?

For grant-funded pilots, agreement typically takes about three weeks from first conversation, and the first cohort is typically in training 6–8 weeks after signing. Counting from the first conversation, Week 1 is a discovery session, Week 2 co-design and Week 3 the agreement; public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions run from outreach and selection to placement support and 12 months of outcome tracking, with the exam fee included; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.
