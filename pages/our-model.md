Delivery Model

## How a Program Is Delivered

The design choices that make the path work for learners who start from different places.

### Accessible Readiness Assessment

Tests and an interview route applicants to a career track or a foundation track. Selection uses a customized psychometric analysis, a written test and an interview; a small number may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

### Foundation Tracks

Learners with limited computer or English skills get extra foundation hours and facilitators, scoped in co-design. Their outcomes are reported by track.

### Blended Training with Certification

Each certification takes 100 training hours (50 hours of lectures and 50 hours of hands-on labs) over 3–6 months. Three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring follow, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client), and then learners join the alumni network.

### Connectivity-Light Delivery

Lessons run on a 1.5 Mbps connection, and 720p lessons can be downloaded for offline viewing.

### Hub-and-Spoke Delivery at Scale

Up to 10 certification cohorts run in parallel from about month 5, in classes of 20 (labs) and 40 (lectures). Larger programs use hub-and-spoke delivery with certified local facilitators.

### Placement Support

Placement support includes assessment-based career advising, CV writing, job-application and interview preparation, industry internships and apprenticeships, job fairs, employer matching and freelance-marketplace onboarding.

Quality

## Quality Assurance

Programs use common practical-assessment rubrics, sampled moderation of portfolios and assessments, and trainer observation, with remediation before the next cohort. Completion, competency and external-exam results are reported separately.

Safeguarding and Data

## Safeguarding and Data Are Agreed per Program

Each program design specifies consent, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response, referral pathways and arrangements for participants under 18. These are agreed and documented before enrollment.
