# CodersTrust Presents at In5 in Dubai

Written in October 2026 from the dated sources linked in the text. Event date: April 29, 2014.

[image: A presenter holding a microphone stands in front of a projected CodersTrust logo slide]

In April 2014, CodersTrust’s leaders spoke at a start-up event at In5, Dubai Internet City, and met investors and potential partners in the Gulf.

The evening, on how to unite and grow the start-up ecosystem in the United Arab Emirates, was held at In5, the innovation hub at Dubai Internet City. The National and Gulf News reported on CodersTrust’s visit at the time, and CodersTrust’s own event page describes the evening.
