# About CodersTrust

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

Mission and Vision

## Why We Exist, and Where We Are Heading

Mission

To transform and empower people facing barriers to opportunity, especially youth and women, with education and work opportunities, to make them financially independent.

Vision

A world with far less youth unemployment, where disadvantaged and marginalized young people gain digital skills and take part in the global digital economy.

The Evidence

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Women offered a place had an estimated 41% higher monthly income (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

Our Story

## From a Copenhagen Launch to National Programs
- 2014

### Launch in Copenhagen

Founded 2014; launched in Copenhagen, Denmark, in the presence of Sir Richard Branson and early Skype investor Morten Lund.
- 2014–2017

### Freelancer Development and Talent Financing

CodersTrust begins developing freelancers in Bangladesh with a talent-financing model; international programs follow.
- 2018–2021

### NGO Workforce Programs and COVID-19 Teacher Training

NGO workforce programs, including WSDFM, and 10,000 teachers trained to teach online during COVID-19.

Government contracts including Her Power, EDGE and SKIT; a partnership with the University for Peace (UPEACE).

JobReady platform rolls out in stages; 1.5M+ National University learners in the onboarding pipeline; YouthWIDE aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth in Bangladesh by 2030 and lets funders deploy the CodersTrust model in a country; Impact Survey 2026.

How We Work

## Four Principles Behind Every Program

### Outcomes over Outputs

We judge a program by whether young people end up working and earning, not by how many attended.

We document results through a baseline at enrollment and follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months; where available, offer letters, payslips and platform or bank records document work and earnings.

See the Outcomes

### Inclusion by Design

Programs are built so that people with fewer computer or English skills can still join, with women a priority in every group.

A readiness assessment, written test and interview guide selection and placement into a career or foundation track; a small number of applicants may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

How Our Model Works

### Evidence Before Claims

We say what a randomized trial shows and what a survey only describes, and we keep the two apart.

The Randomized Trial

### Partnership and Accountability

Partners agree the scorecard before the first cohort and see progress in reports every month.

Funders receive monthly reports: enrollment & attendance, completion & certification, placements & first earnings, tracer results (3/6/12 months), women and priority-group shares, trainee satisfaction; quarterly steering review; independent evaluation welcome.
