About Us

# A Global Workforce Organization, Built on Evidence

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

See Our Impact

[image: A speaker addressing a European Parliament committee room]

Mission and Vision

## Why We Exist

Mission

To transform and empower people facing barriers to opportunity, especially youth and women, with education and work opportunities, to make them financially independent.

Vision

A world with far less youth unemployment, where disadvantaged and marginalized young people gain digital skills and take part in the global digital economy.

Our Story

## From a Copenhagen Launch to National Programs
- 2014

### Launch in Copenhagen

Founded 2014; launched in Copenhagen, Denmark, in the presence of Sir Richard Branson and early Skype investor Morten Lund.
- 2014–2017

### Freelancer Development and Talent Financing

CodersTrust begins developing freelancers in Bangladesh with a talent-financing model; international programs follow, including Kosovo with the World Bank Group.
- 2018–2021

### NGO Workforce Programs and COVID-19 Teacher Training

NGO workforce programs, including WSDFM, and 10,000 teachers trained to teach online during COVID-19.
- 2022–2024

### Government Contracts and University Partnerships

Government contracts including Her Power, EDGE and SKIT; partnerships with National University (official training partner since 2023) and the University for Peace (UPEACE).
- 2025–2026

### JobReady, YouthWIDE and the Impact Survey

JobReady platform rolls out in stages; YouthWIDE aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth in Bangladesh by 2030 and lets funders deploy the CodersTrust model in a country; Impact Survey 2026.

How We Work

## Four Principles Behind Every Program

### Outcomes over Outputs

We judge a program by whether young people end up working and earning, not by how many attended.

See the Outcomes

### Inclusion by Design

Programs are built so that people with fewer computer or English skills can still join, with women a priority in every group.

How Our Model Works

### Evidence Before Claims

We say what a randomized trial shows and what a survey only describes, and we keep the two apart.

The Randomized Trial

### Partnership and Accountability

As agreed in the program scope, partners set the scorecard before the first cohort and receive monthly reports.
