# About CodersTrust

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

Our Story

## Built Since 2014

CodersTrust launched in 2014 in Copenhagen and began developing freelancers in Bangladesh. Our first UNDP-supported pilot (2015) was in Dhaka’s Korail settlement.

Mission and Vision

## Why We Exist, and Where We Are Heading

Mission

To transform and empower underprivileged and marginalized people, especially youth and women, with education and work opportunities, to make them financially independent.

Vision

By 2032, to be among the top three digital education and employment platforms globally, focused on developing and emerging markets — offering localized digital education and training, job-ready skilled workers and knowledge-based employment for the next billion global youth.

How We Work

## Four Principles Behind Every Program

### Outcomes over Outputs

We judge a program by whether young people end up working and earning, not by how many attended.

Results documented with a baseline at enrollment, tracers at 3/6/12 months, payslips/offer letters, platform logs and bank receipts for freelancers.

See the Outcomes

### Inclusion by Design

Programs are built so that people with fewer computer or English skills can still join, with women a priority in every group.

Accessible readiness assessment: tests and an interview route applicants to a career track or a foundation track; selection uses a customized psychometric analysis, a written test and an interview; a small number may not be selected, and the reasons are reported.

How Our Model Works

### Evidence Before Claims

We say what a randomized trial shows and what a survey only describes, and we keep the two apart.

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

The Randomized Trial

### Partnership and Accountability

Partners agree the scorecard before the first cohort and see progress in reports every month.

Funders receive monthly reports: enrollment & attendance, completion & certification, placements & first earnings, tracer results (3/6/12 months), women and priority-group shares, trainee satisfaction; quarterly steering review; independent evaluation welcome.
