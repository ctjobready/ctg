About Us

# A Global Workforce Organization, Built on Evidence

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

CodersTrust is headquartered in New York, and its parent organization is CT USA, Inc. CodersTrust Bangladesh (CodersTrust Bangladesh Ltd.) is a country office. Additional country and regional offices are planned.

Mission and Vision

## Why We Exist

Mission

To transform and empower people facing barriers to opportunity, especially youth and women, with education and work opportunities, to make them financially independent.

Vision

A world with far less youth unemployment, where disadvantaged and marginalized young people gain digital skills and take part in the global digital economy.

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed.

Our Story

## From a Copenhagen Launch to National Programs
- 2014

### Launch in Copenhagen

Founded in 2014 and launched in Copenhagen, Denmark, in the presence of Sir Richard Branson and early Skype investor Morten Lund.
- 2014–2017

### Freelancer Development and Talent Financing

CodersTrust begins training freelancers in Bangladesh with a talent-financing model; international programs follow, including Kosovo with the World Bank Group.
- 2015–2016

### Rockefeller Foundation Grant

A Rockefeller Foundation grant supported a CodersTrust feasibility study in Kenya and Nigeria.
- 2018–2021

### NGO Workforce Programs and COVID-19 Teacher Training

NGO workforce programs, including WSDFM, and 10,000 teachers trained to teach online during COVID-19.
- 2023–2025

### Government Contracts and University Partnerships

Government contracts in Bangladesh, including Her Power, EDGE (Enhancing Digital Government and Economy) and SKIT; partnerships with National University (official training partner since 2023) and the University for Peace (UPEACE).
- 2025–2026

### JobReady, YouthWIDE and the Impact Survey

JobReady rolls out in stages. YouthWIDE aims to train 1 million educated youth (Higher Secondary Certificate or above) for digital employment by 2030, in Bangladesh and similar countries in South Asia and the Middle East and North Africa, depending on government and donor commitments; CodersTrust publishes Impact Survey 2026.

## Four Principles Behind Every Program

### Outcomes over Outputs

We judge youth-employment programs by whether young people end up working and earning, not by how many attended.

See the Outcomes

### Inclusion by Design

Supported youth cohorts are built so that people with fewer computer or English skills can still join, with women a priority in every group.

How Our Model Works

### Evidence Before Claims

We say what a randomized trial shows and what a survey only describes, and we keep the two apart.

The Randomized Trial

### Partnership and Accountability

As agreed in the program scope, partners set the scorecard before the first cohort and receive monthly reports.
