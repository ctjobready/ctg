Five Programs

## Find the Program That Fits Where You Start
- ### YouthWIDE

The youth digital-employment program, designed as co-funded country cohorts. It aims to train 1 million educated youth by 2030, depending on government and donor commitments.

Best For: Governments, Development Partners and Foundations

For Governments

For Development Partners

For Foundations

In Practice

Ministries can follow phased delivery from training to paid work, and funders get monthly reports on progress toward paid work, not only enrollment, as agreed in the program scope.
- ### JobReady@Campus

Industry-oriented skills training in parallel with academic studies, using on-campus labs.

Best For: Universities and Colleges

For Universities

In Practice

Students get a supported route to industry certification beside the degree.
- ### JobReady@Work

Corporate training custom-developed for each organization, plus a route to hire job-ready talent.

Best For: Employers

For Employers

In Practice

Your team learns on tasks from your own roles.
- ### NU Post Graduate Diploma

National University post graduate diploma courses delivered by CodersTrust, in digital marketing, information and communication technology (ICT), cyber security and data analytics.

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25

Explore the NU Post Graduate Diploma

In Practice

You add digital skills to the qualification you hold.
- ### SuperKids

STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art for young learners.

Best For: Schools and Education Partners

Explore SuperKids

In Practice

A school can bring these into the classroom, so children build digital skills early.
