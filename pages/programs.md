Five Routes

## Find the Program That Fits Where You Start
- ### YouthWIDE

The youth digital-employment program, run as co-funded country cohorts, with outcome evidence so far from Bangladesh and Kosovo. In Bangladesh it aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth for digital employment by 2030, depending on government and donor commitments.

Best For: Governments, Development Partners and Funders

## Trial and Survey Evidence

Trial figures are for a predecessor program. Survey figures are among surveyed completers, from the October 2026 survey.

### An Independent Randomized Trial

For every 100 women offered a place, about 10 more were in work at follow-up than among 100 comparable women in the control group (+10.3 percentage points).

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.
