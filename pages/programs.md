CodersTrust offers four partner programs for institutions — YouthWIDE, JobReady@Campus, JobReady@Work and SuperKids — and the NU Postgraduate Diploma for individual learners.

Five Programs

## Find the Program That Fits Where You Start
- ### YouthWIDE

The youth digital-employment program, designed as co-funded country cohorts for South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa. It builds on results from earlier programs in Bangladesh and Kosovo. In Bangladesh it aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth for digital employment by 2030, depending on government and donor commitments.

Best For: Governments, Development Partners and Funders

- ### JobReady@Campus

Industry-oriented skills training in parallel with academic studies, using on-campus labs.

Best For: Universities and Colleges

For Universities

In Practice

- ### JobReady@Work

Corporate training custom-developed for each organization, plus access to job-ready talent.

Best For: Employers

For Employers

In Practice

Your team learns on tasks from your own roles.
- ### NU Postgraduate Diploma

National University postgraduate diploma courses delivered by CodersTrust, in digital marketing and ICT.

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25

In Practice

You add digital skills to the qualification you hold.
- ### SuperKids

STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art for young learners.

Best For: Schools and Education Partners

In Practice

A school can bring STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art into the classroom.
