CodersTrust offers four partner programs for institutions — YouthWIDE, JobReady@Campus, JobReady@Work and SuperKids — and the NU Postgraduate Diploma for degree holders.

## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

## Choose the Program That Fits Your Goal

Each card says who the program is for and what it means in practice.
- ### YouthWIDE

The youth digital-employment program. In Bangladesh it is a national initiative that aims to train 2 million vulnerable youth for digital employment by 2030, a national ambition that depends on government and donor commitments. In other countries it runs as co-funded country cohorts; current outcome evidence comes from Bangladesh and Kosovo.

Best For: Governments, Development Partners and Funders

Which means

ministries can follow phased delivery to young people in work, and funders get monthly reports designed to track paid-work outcomes, not only enrollment.
- ### JobReady@Campus

Industry-oriented skills training in parallel with academic studies, using on-campus labs.

Best For: Universities and Colleges

Which means

students get a supported route to a recognized credential beside the degree.
- ### JobReady@Work

Corporate training custom-developed for each organization, plus access to job-ready talent.

Best For: Employers

Which means

your team learns on tasks from your own roles.
- ### NU Postgraduate Diploma

National University postgraduate diploma courses delivered by CodersTrust, in digital marketing and ICT.

Best For: Degree Holders in Bangladesh

Which means

you add digital skills to the degree you hold.
- ### SuperKids

STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art for young learners.

Best For: Schools and Education Partners

a school can bring STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art into the classroom.

The Path

## How YouthWIDE and JobReady@Campus Cohorts Work

Each program adapts the same three steps to its partner.
- ### Step 1: Discover and Co-Design

A discovery session sets priority groups, tracks, the indicative budget and the pilot scorecard; each track’s demand and work-entry plan is approved before enrollment.
- ### Step 2: Train and Mentor

100 training hours per certification, then three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client).
- ### Step 3: Place and Report

Placement support, monthly reports and tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months.

## Trial and Survey Evidence

Trial figures are for a predecessor program. Survey figures are among surveyed completers, from the October 2026 survey.

### An Independent Randomized Trial

For every 100 women offered a place, about 10 more were in work at follow-up than among 100 comparable women in the control group (+10.3 percentage points).

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.
