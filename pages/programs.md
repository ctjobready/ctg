# CodersTrust Programs

CodersTrust runs five programs, each for a different partner: funders and governments, universities, employers, degree holders and schools.

For governments and funders: YouthWIDE. For universities and colleges: JobReady@Campus. For employers: JobReady@Work. For degree holders: NU Postgraduate Diploma. For schools: SuperKids.

## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

The youth digital-employment program. The Path

## How Supported Employment Cohorts Work

In YouthWIDE and JobReady@Campus cohorts, each program adapts the same three steps to its partner.
- ### Step 1: Discover and Co-Design

A discovery session sets priority groups, tracks, the indicative budget and the pilot scorecard; each track’s demand and work-entry plan is approved before enrollment.
- ### Step 2: Train and Mentor

100 training hours per certification, then three months of milestone-based one-to-one mentoring, with placement support alongside, toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal (for freelance tracks: profile live, first bids, first client).
- ### Step 3: Place and Report

Placement support, monthly reports and tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months.

The Evidence

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.
