# Bring Coding, Robotics and Digital Art into Your Classroom

SuperKids brings block coding, robotics and graphic design for kids into schools, with courses shaped with you in a discovery session, so children build digital skills early.

Decision Summary

## SuperKids at a Glance

Who It Is For

Schools and education partners that want young learners to build digital skills early, through block coding, robotics, and graphic design for kids, shaped with you in a discovery session.

Delivered So Far

400+ SuperKids learners since 2021.

Agreements

Agreements with the Department of ICT (DoICT) and the National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB); details below.

## Options Are Shaped with You in a Discovery Session

Bring these three things to the conversation.

### Your School or Organization

Location, age groups and the approximate number of learners.

### Your Goals

Which of STEAM, coding, robotics and digital art you want to start with.

### Your Timeline

When you would like to begin, and two suitable times for a conversation.

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session.
