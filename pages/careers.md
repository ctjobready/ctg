How to Apply

## How Applications Work

Applications go to the CodersTrust Bangladesh HR team.
- ### Step 1: Your Application

Send your resume. We keep it on file for when a role opens.
- ### Step 2: Application Review

When a role opens, we review the applications on file and invite shortlisted candidates to interview.
- ### Step 3: Employment Offer

The selected candidate receives an offer letter and package details.

The button opens a prefilled email to career@coderstrustbd.com; you can also send your resume to that address yourself.
