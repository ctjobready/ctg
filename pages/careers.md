How We Work

## What to Expect

### Inclusive and Flexible

A workplace that is active, flexible, diverse and inclusive, socially responsible and open to new thinking.

### Work That Is Measured

Our programs are judged by whether young people end up working and earning, not by how many attended.

### Wins Are Celebrated

Big or small, we celebrate the wins, because we know how hard the team works.

How to Apply

## From CV to Offer

Applications go to the CodersTrust Bangladesh HR team.

- 1 Step 1: Resume Assessment We scan and sort resumes to find the best candidates.
- 2 Step 2: In-Person Interview Shortlisted candidates are invited to a face-to-face interview.
- 3 Step 3: Employment Offer The selected candidate receives an offer letter and package details.

Send your CV to career@coderstrustbd.com .

Equal Opportunity

## Everyone Is Equal Here

We believe every person is equal, and we judge people on their work and merit. We put inclusion at the front of how we operate and build tolerance and understanding into everything we do.
