What You Get

## What Your Cohort Grant Gets
- ### Mentoring, Placement Support and Outcome Tracking

Mentoring and placement support are standard inclusions, and outcomes are tracked for 12 months after each course. Results are measured against the agreed scorecard, including first paid work, not attendance alone.
- ### A Pilot Scorecard Agreed with You

Cost per completer and per verified outcome, with thresholds agreed before the first cohort starts.
- ### Progress You Can See Every Month

Monthly reports and quarterly reviews keep progress visible. Where agreed, SDG-mapped reporting follows your CSR framework, with co-branded certificates and events.

Questions, Answered

## Questions About Cohort Grants

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of work and earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a partnership includes.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. See how we handle them.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.

## What Happens Next

For platform grants, the first conversation covers JobReady’s infrastructure and staged rollout. For cohort grants, the steps are below.
- ### Step 1: Discovery (Week 1)

A conversation about your goals, priority groups and timeline.
- ### Step 2: Co-Design (Week 2)

We shape tracks, cohorts, safeguarding and reporting with you.
- ### Step 3: Agreement (Week 3)

Scope, schedule and the pilot scorecard are agreed.
- ### Step 4: Training Starts 6–8 Weeks After Signing

The first cohort begins training; monthly reports follow.
- Your first step: a discovery session. In your inquiry, include two suitable times for a discovery conversation.
