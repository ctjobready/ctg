The Cost of Waiting

## Every Year of Delay Leaves a Cohort Without a Route to Work

### Educated Young People Are Waiting

An estimated 62 million young people aged 15–24 with higher-secondary education or more (range 61–78 million) are not in employment, education or training across South Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa.

What It Means

Every year of delay is a cohort without a path.

### Training Does Not Always Lead to Employment

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Look for programs that build in placement, tracking and evaluation.

What You Get

## What Your Cohort Grant Gets
- ### Mentoring, Placement Support and Outcome Tracking

Mentoring and placement support are standard inclusions, and outcomes are tracked for 12 months after each course. Results are measured against first paid work, not attendance, using the agreed scorecard.
- ### A Pilot Scorecard Agreed with You

Cost per completer and per verified outcome, with thresholds agreed before the first cohort starts.
- ### Progress You Can See Every Month

Monthly reports, a quarterly steering review and a pilot scorecard, with the contracting entity and due-diligence documents confirmed in co-design. Where agreed in the program scope: SDG-mapped reporting tailored to your CSR framework, and co-branded certificates and events.

Objections, Answered

## Questions About Cohort Grants

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. See what a partnership includes.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. See how we handle them.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing as further levels are published.
