## What Happens Next

- 1 Step 1: Your Inquiry In your inquiry, include two suitable times for a discovery conversation.
- 2 Step 2: A Conversation We talk through your goals, priority groups and timeline.
- 3 Step 3: A First Cohort, Designed with You We shape tracks, safeguarding and reporting together.

- For grant-funded pilots, your first cohort is in training 6–8 weeks after signing, and monthly reports follow from the first cohort.
