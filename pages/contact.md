## For Program and Funding Inquiries
- ### Step 1: Your Inquiry

In your inquiry, include two suitable times for a discovery conversation.
- ### Step 2: A Conversation

We talk through your goals, priority groups and timeline.
- ### Step 3: A First Cohort, Designed with You

We shape tracks, safeguarding and reporting together.
- For grant-funded pilots, we plan for the first cohort to begin training 6–8 weeks after signing, and monthly reports follow from the first cohort.
- Public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.
