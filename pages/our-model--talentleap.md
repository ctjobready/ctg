# TalentLEAP: A Skill-by-Skill Path from Learning to Earning

TalentLEAP organizes learning around assessed skill units and career paths. The first published TalentLEAP level is Competent; Proficient and Expert levels are planned. Learners move through three stages, Learn, Earn and Prosper, with three months of milestone-based mentoring after the course.

## How TalentLEAP Works

### The Stage Pipeline
- Learn
- Step 1, TalentLEAP stage Learn

#### Assess

A readiness assessment routes applicants to a career or foundation track.
- Step 2, TalentLEAP stage Learn

#### Learn

Skill units on a track set from employer demand, with hands-on labs.
- Step 3, TalentLEAP stage Learn

#### Prepare for Certification

Certification exam preparation (exam fees and attempts as agreed in the program scope).
- Earn
- Step 4, TalentLEAP stage Earn

#### Earn

Milestone-based mentoring and placement support toward the track’s work-entry goal.
- Prosper
- Step 5, TalentLEAP stage Prosper

#### Prosper

Career paths, continuing development and the alumni network. Further TalentLEAP levels are planned.

Assess, Learn and Prepare for Certification make up the Learn stage; Earn and Prosper follow.

## Example: The Digital Marketing Career Path

TalentLEAP organizes skills into career paths with progressive levels. In this example the levels are Competent, Proficient and Expert.
- Level 1 · Competent

### Introduction to Digital Marketing with Google and Meta Certifications
- Three months of mentorship by an experienced professional
- Three months of membership in a digital-marketing expertise community
- Bonus mini-courses in essential business and leadership skills
- Proficient and Expert levels are planned.

CodersTrust is not affiliated with or endorsed by these certifying bodies; it prepares learners for their exams.

Example only: the Digital Marketing career path. Competent is the published first level.

## What Makes TalentLEAP Different
- Competency-based rather than time-based. Learning is organized around individual skill units that are assessed, not around hours attended alone.
- Milestone-based mentoring. Mentoring follows milestones toward each track’s agreed work-entry goal, with placement support alongside, and attendance is tracked.
- Demand-led tracks. Tracks are set from current employer demand in each country, and each track’s work-entry plan is agreed before enrollment.

## Questions About TalentLEAP

### How Is CodersTrust Different from an Online Course Platform?

Compared with a self-paced course alone, supported CodersTrust cohorts add mentoring, certification exam preparation and placement support, agreed in each program’s scope. As agreed in the program scope, every supported TalentLEAP cohort pairs 100 training hours per certification with three months of milestone-based mentoring, certification exam preparation (with exam fees and attempts), placement support and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring.

### Is There Independent Evidence?

Yes, for CodersTrust’s WSDFM program. The BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) at BRAC University ran a randomized controlled trial of that 200-hour program, longer than today’s 100-hour tracks, with women in Dhaka trained in 2020. Women offered a place were more likely to be employed than the control group. The trial is also published in a peer-reviewed journal: Arman, Bhattacharjee, Das, Gomes and Mahmood (2026), “Tackling Women’s Unemployment: Evidence from an Online Jobs Experiment in Bangladesh”, Economic Development and Cultural Change 75(1): 367–399, https://doi.org/10.1086/741450
