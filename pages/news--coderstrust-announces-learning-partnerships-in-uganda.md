# CodersTrust Announces Learning Partnerships in Uganda

Written in October 2026 from the dated sources linked in the text. Event date: September 10, 2020.

In September 2020, CodersTrust Global announced learning partnerships in Uganda with Maarifasasa, Makerere University Business School and U-Touch.

The release of September 10, 2020 describes partnerships that combine online and blended classroom learning with local mentors. It also announced partnerships in Benin, with SOS Children’s Villages; in Egypt, with CrossWorkers; and in Somaliland. A Makerere University Business School page on its ICT unit says the school partners with CodersTrust Global Academy to offer the skills most in demand in freelancing, and Maarifasasa published a video about the partnership.
