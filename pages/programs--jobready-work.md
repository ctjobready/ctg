Objections, Answered

## Questions Employers Ask

### Is JobReady@Work Right for Us?

Right for you if you want tracks built around the skills your roles need. Talent requests are scoped against the experience and credentials available in the candidate pool.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are exposed, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### Do Your Learners Only Freelance?

No — salaried jobs remain the largest pathway; freelancing and business ownership grew the most in relative terms. Among surveyed completers, salaried employment rose from 38.3% before training to 53.3% at the October 2026 survey, freelancing from 6.2% to 14.0% and business ownership from 2.8% to 10.6%.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program and shared after the needs assessment. Standard inclusions are a needs assessment, a customized curriculum, interactive workshops, progress evaluation and post-training support; certification exam preparation and talent sourcing are scoped separately where needed.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer and gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.
