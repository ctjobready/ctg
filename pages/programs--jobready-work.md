## A Program Built Around Your Roles

Our Approach

For employers who need teams ready to use AI in their daily work, CodersTrust offers JobReady@Work, training custom-developed for each organization. Each program starts with a needs assessment of your roles, is delivered through workshops on tasks from those roles, and is checked against success measures you agree before you start.

## Questions About the Program

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session and confirm it after the needs assessment. Standard inclusions are a needs assessment, a customized curriculum, interactive workshops, progress evaluation and post-training support; certification exam preparation and talent sourcing are scoped separately where needed.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer marketplace and the gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.
