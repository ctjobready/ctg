## JobReady@Work at a Glance

Who It Is For

Employers who want their teams upskilled for AI workflows, and job-ready digital talent for open roles, introduced through CodersTrust’s placement team and scoped against the experience and credentials available.

Training

Soft-skills and hard-skills tracks, built around the tasks in your own roles; AI leadership programs with AIvolve. Certification preparation where a track offers one, with exam eligibility and attempts agreed in the program scope.

Measuring Progress

Post-training support and a progress evaluation against the measures you set, for team capability in the digital and AI tasks defined in the needs assessment.

## Questions About the Program

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session and confirm it after the needs assessment. Standard inclusions are a needs assessment, a customized curriculum, interactive workshops, progress evaluation and post-training support; certification exam preparation and talent sourcing are scoped separately where needed.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partner portals and AI chat are live; the employer marketplace and the gig-work marketplace are in pre-launch. Hiring currently runs through CodersTrust’s placement team.
