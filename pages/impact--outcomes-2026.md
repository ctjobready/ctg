# Impact Survey 2026: Surveyed Completers Report More Work and Higher Income

Among surveyed completers, employment rose from 47.4% before training to 77.9% at the October 2026 survey, and median monthly income rose from $82 to $245. These are what respondents report: the survey describes outcomes among alumni who answered, and it does not show that training caused the change.

## Who Responded

Nearly half of respondents were employed before training (47.4%). Respondent profile: 84.6% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher; 68.7% aged 15–35; 19.9% women; 62.9% live outside Dhaka; 58 of 64 districts; 16.0% rural.

Employment results rest on n = 321 paired answers and income results on n = 277.

## How to Read This Survey

### Methodology and Limitations
- Survey. CodersTrust Impact Survey, October 2026: an online alumni survey, fielded in October 2026.
- Who the respondents are. 84.6% of respondents hold a bachelor’s degree or higher, so results describe a mostly graduate group.
- What it does not show. Respondents are not a random sample and may over-represent successful learners, so the survey does not show that training caused any change.
