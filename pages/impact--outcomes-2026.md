# Surveyed Completers Report More Work and Higher Income

Among surveyed completers, employment rose from 48.0% before training to 78.2% at the October 2026 survey, and median monthly income rose from $98 to $245. These are what respondents report: the survey describes outcomes among alumni who answered, and it does not show that training caused the change.

Source snapshot: October 2026 · Last updated October 29, 2026

## Who Responded

Nearly half of respondents were employed before training (48.0%). Respondent profile: 85.0% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher; 68.2% aged 15–35; 19.6% women; 63.0% live outside Dhaka; 58 of 64 districts; 15.6% rural.

Employment results rest on n = 331 paired answers and income results on n = 286.

## How Fast Earnings Started

Surveyed new earners are the completers who first earned during or after training.

## How to Read This Survey

### Methodology and Limitations
- Survey. CodersTrust Impact Survey, October 2026: an online alumni survey, fielded in October 2026.
- Who the respondents are. 85.0% of respondents hold a bachelor’s degree or higher and 19.6% are women, so results describe a mostly graduate, majority-male group.
- What it does not show. Respondents are not a random sample and may over-represent successful learners, so the survey does not show that training caused any change.
