# CodersTrust Chairman Aziz Ahmad Meets Bangladesh Foreign Affairs Minister Khalilur Rahman

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published September 13, 2026; updated October 30, 2026.

[image: Two men in suits posing together indoors]

Mr. Aziz Ahmad, Co-Founder and Chairman of CodersTrust, held a warm and insightful courtesy meeting with the Hon’ble Foreign Minister of Bangladesh, who is also President of the UN General Assembly.

The discussion focused on CodersTrust’s mission to empower Bangladesh’s youth with future-ready digital skills, while supporting the country’s growing role in the global digital economy. They also emphasized the importance of public-private partnerships, global collaboration and technology-driven education in creating sustainable opportunities for employment and entrepreneurship.

As Bangladesh continues its digital transformation, CodersTrust remains committed to developing a skilled, globally competitive workforce and unlocking the potential of the nation’s youth.
