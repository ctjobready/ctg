## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

Larger programs run hub-and-spoke through certified local facilitators and existing labs and colleges, and CodersTrust Bangladesh supports local partners with curriculum, certification, mentoring, reporting and safeguarding.

Where government lab access is confirmed, cohorts can run in public digital labs; your organization can also host cohorts in its own labs, classrooms or centers.

The Cost of Waiting

## Cohorts That Train but Do Not Place Cost Community Trust.

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT’s Release.

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Skills that were enough last year may not open doors next year, so tracks have to follow current demand.

### A Review of Nine Trials Found Only Three with Significant Employment Impacts.

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Placement and tracking have to be built into the program, not added afterward.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

## How a Local Partnership Works

Where We Stand

For Bangladeshi NGOs, colleges and chambers who need to move young people in their communities into paid digital work, CodersTrust offers an integrated training-to-earnings model delivered through certified local facilitators and existing labs. ## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

## How It Works in Three Steps
- ### Step 1: Tell the Bangladesh Team

Describe your organization, your district and the groups you work with, and we shape the cohort, tracks, safeguarding and reporting together.
- ### Step 2: Host, Train and Mentor

Certified local facilitators deliver the localized curriculum in your labs, classrooms or centers: 100 training hours per certification, then three months of milestone-based mentoring.
- ### Step 3: Place and Report

Placement support into local, remote and global work, with monthly reports to the funder, where a funder is involved, and tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months.

## Questions Local Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our District?

Each cohort is designed around the young people your organization reaches, your facilities and local demand. Tracks, facilitator training, safeguarding and reporting are agreed before enrollment.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. Each program design covers accessible selection, consent-based data collection, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response and referral pathways, with guardian consent and supervision for any participant under 18; funders receive privacy-appropriate data.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.
