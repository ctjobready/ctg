## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

Larger programs run through certified local facilitators in existing labs and colleges, coordinated centrally, and CodersTrust Bangladesh supports local partners with curriculum, certification, mentoring, reporting and safeguarding.

## Cohorts That Train Without Placement Cost Community Trust

### On One Major Freelancing Platform, Automation-Prone Postings Fell After ChatGPT’s Release

Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding jobs on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive jobs, and image-creation postings fell 17% within about a year of image-generating AI.

What It Means

Skills that were enough last year may not open doors next year, so training has to follow current demand.

### A Review of Nine Trials Found Only Three with Significant Employment Impacts

A review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

What It Means

Placement and tracking have to be built into the program, not added afterward.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

The Evidence

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD) found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher than the control group’s (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher than the control group’s.

Employment at follow-up: 52.8% in the control group and 63.1% for women offered a place.

## What Your Community Gets
- ### Certified Facilitators and a Local Curriculum

Certified-facilitator training, a localized curriculum and preparation for industry certification. Local capacity stays after a funded cohort ends, and you do not have to build the curriculum or certification route yourself.
- ### Mentoring and Placement Support

Three months of milestone-based mentoring, with placement support alongside, gives your completers a route into work that your organization does not have to build alone.
- ### Monthly Reports and Program Updates

Where a funder is involved, it receives monthly reports, and your organization receives agreed program updates on enrollment, completion, certification and placement.

Objections, Answered

## Questions Local Partners Ask

### Will This Work in Our District?

Each cohort is designed around the young people your organization reaches, your facilities and local demand. Tracks, facilitator training, safeguarding and reporting are agreed before enrollment.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. See how we handle them.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing as further levels are published.
