### What Will I Be Able to Do After This Course?

Build a foundation in analytics tools, databases, data visualization and machine learning, then apply it in the final special-topics thesis or project report. Your next step depends on your experience, portfolio and local market.

### Is This the Right Course for Me?

Eligibility: Honors / Diploma holders with a minimum CGPA of 2.25. Choose Data Analytics if you want to study analytics tools, databases, data visualization and machine learning. Not right if you want a short self-paced course: see JobReady.global.
