How It Works

## JobReady@Campus: Training Alongside the Degree

Where We Stand

For universities and colleges who need their degree holders in paid digital work, CodersTrust offers an integrated training-to-earnings model that runs alongside the degree. Unlike approaches that typically focus on one part of the path — training-only programs, self-paced course platforms or freelance marketplaces alone (provision varies by provider) — we design, deliver and track the whole path to earnings, working in phases through existing labs and colleges, with outcome evidence that includes a randomized trial by BIGD.

- 1 Set Up Campus Labs as Learning Centers On-campus labs become blended-learning centers with certified local facilitators; faculty members act as coordinators.
- 2 Certify Industry Certification Tracks 100 training hours per certification, with certification exam preparation; exam eligibility, attempts and terms are agreed in the program scope.
- 3 Mentor and Place Mentoring and Placement Three months of milestone-based mentoring and placement support, with outcome tracking for 12 months.
- 4 Alongside Studies In Parallel with the Degree Training runs in parallel with academic studies, with professional internships.

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

## Questions Universities Ask

Right for you if you want employer-demand tracks with certification preparation alongside degrees. Not right if you want to replace the degree.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### Who Are Your Typical Learners?

Mostly university graduates from across Bangladesh, some of them already working before training. Among surveyed completers, 84.6% hold a bachelor’s degree or higher, 68.7% were aged 15–35 and 62.9% live outside Dhaka, across 58 of the country’s 64 districts; 47.4% were working before training.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Evidence includes payslips and offer letters for jobs, platform logs or bank receipts for freelancing; requirements are agreed in co-design. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

What the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP organizes skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

## Start with a Pilot. Decide on Evidence.

A pilot scorecard is agreed in co-design: completion, certification, first paid work, sustained work (for example, earnings in at least three of six months above an agreed minimum), and cost per completer and per verified outcome. Missed thresholds trigger a corrective plan, expansion is the funder’s decision, and independent evaluation is welcome.
