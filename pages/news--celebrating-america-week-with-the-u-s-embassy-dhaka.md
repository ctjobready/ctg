# Celebrating America Week with the U.S. Embassy Dhaka

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published September 2, 2026; updated October 29, 2026.

[image: Two men standing in front of an American flag and a Bangladeshi flag]

CodersTrust was proudly represented by CodersTrust Bangladesh CEO Md. Shamsul Haque, former Additional Foreign Secretary of Bangladesh, and his team at Ambassador Brent T. Christensen’s Cocktail Reception in appreciation of the National Day and America Week. This moment builds on an incredible year of partnership with the Embassy—including our Great American Treasure Hunt, which lit up Dhaka with 1,400+ registered users and 15,000+ QR scans celebrating America’s 250th anniversary!

[image: An invitation card to a U.S. Embassy reception beside two group photographs]

None of this happens without our visionary Chairman, Aziz Ahmad, whose relentless drive continues to shape CodersTrust into a force for uplifting youths globally.

Grateful for this partnership, and excited for what’s next.
