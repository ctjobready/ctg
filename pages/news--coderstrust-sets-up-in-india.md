# CodersTrust Sets Up in India

Archived post, lightly edited. Figures, job titles and references to officeholders reflect the date of publication; current figures are on the Impact pages. Published August 7, 2016.

Added in October 2026 from the dated sources linked in the text.

CodersTrust began operating in India in 2015 and had an office in Gurugram in 2016.

In October 2015, Tech in Asia wrote, in a report reprinted by NextBillion, that CodersTrust had recently launched initiatives in India and parts of Africa, while most of its work was in Bangladesh. On June 29, 2016, The Economic Times reported that the company had set up operations in India and was moving to Pune to make it its hub for the region. On August 7, 2016, The Times of India featured the head of CodersTrust’s India operations in a story about how low-income youth are learning to code.
