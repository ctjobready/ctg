### Women’s Skill Development for Freelancing Marketplace (WSDFM)

Commissioned by Porticus

ICT skills for freelancing marketplaces and local jobs, plus women’s empowerment, in Bangladesh.

[image: A woman learner seated at a computer among other learners in a Women in Online Work training room in Kosovo]

102 of 150

women in work (68%) in Kosovo

### Women in Online Work, Kosovo

World Bank Group, 2017

A pilot that tested whether online work suits young women in Kosovo.

[image: Young trainees standing together for a group photograph in a training room, from the UNDP YES (Youth Employment through Skills) program]

67%

of the 350 trained found their first job within 3 months

### UNDP YES, Korail

UNDP, Government of Bangladesh, Swanirvar Bangladesh

Skills training for young people from the Korail settlement in Dhaka, linked to freelance marketplaces.

[image: Women working on laptops in a Her Power training lab]

372 of 800

women in work (47%) in the Her Power training cohort

### Her Power

ICT Division / Department of ICT (DoICT), Government of Bangladesh

A national program for women’s self-employment through digital skills, with CodersTrust as curriculum and training partner.

## Women-Focused Programs, Side by Side

Women-focused programs report their own results: WSDFM 71% in work; Women in Online Work, Kosovo 68%; Her Power 47% of its training cohort.
