## Four Programs, Results First

A case study reports a program’s result and its base. A success story is one learner’s own account.

[image: Women trainees and a CodersTrust Bangladesh banner at a WSDFM training session]

### Women’s Skill Development for Freelancing Marketplace (WSDFM)

Partner & Place

Commissioned by Porticus; Bangladesh

Who

Women in Bangladesh

Scope & Delivery

ICT skills for freelancing marketplaces and local jobs, with one-to-one mentoring

Result

Delivered

Program Record

711 of 1,000

WSDFM program records: women in work (71%)

[image: A woman learner seated at a computer among other learners in a Women in Online Work training room in Kosovo]

### Women in Online Work, Kosovo

Partner & Place

World Bank Group; Kosovo, 2017

Who

Young women

Scope & Delivery

Two-phase delivery that built digital literacy first, then real online work

Result

Delivered

Program Record

102 of 150

Women in Online Work, Kosovo: 68% in work

[image: Young trainees standing together for a group photograph in a training room, from the UNDP YES (Youth Employment through Skills) program]

### UNDP YES, Korail

Partner & Place

UNDP, the Government of Bangladesh and Swanirvar Bangladesh; Korail settlement, Dhaka, 2017

Who

Young people from the Korail settlement

Scope & Delivery

Skills training linked to freelance marketplaces, in a fully equipped lab with one-to-one mentoring

Result

Delivered

Program Record

67%

of the 350 trained found their first job within 3 months

[image: Women working on laptops in a Her Power training lab]

### Her Power

Partner & Place

ICT Division / DoICT, Government of Bangladesh; Bangladesh

Who

Women

Scope & Delivery

Curriculum, training manuals, mentorship guidelines and trainer training; a cohort trained directly

Result

Delivered

Program Record

372 of 800

tracked women, from the 2,500 trained directly by CodersTrust, in work: 47%

Women-Focused Programs

## WSDFM, Women in Online Work and Her Power, Side by Side

These three programs serve women. Each reports its own result against its own base, so read them side by side, not as one total.
| Program | Result | Base |
| WSDFM | 71% in work | 711 of 1,000 women |
| Women in Online Work, Kosovo | 68% in work | 102 of 150 women |
| Her Power | 47% in work | 372 of 800 tracked women, from the 2,500 trained directly by CodersTrust |

UNDP YES, Korail is not in this table because it reports a different measure: 67% of the 350 trained found their first job within 3 months, not the share in work at a follow-up.
