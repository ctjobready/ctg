## Questions Employers Ask

### Will This Work in Our Country?

We start with a needs assessment of your roles, tasks and required tools. For hiring, talent requests are scoped against the experience and credentials available in the candidate pool.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session and confirm it after the needs assessment. Standard inclusions are a needs assessment, a customized curriculum, interactive workshops, progress evaluation and post-training support; certification exam preparation and talent sourcing are scoped separately where needed.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, customer relationship management and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer marketplace and the gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.
