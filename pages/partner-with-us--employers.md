## Questions Employers Ask

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are vulnerable to automation, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country. Within eight months of ChatGPT’s release, postings for automation-prone writing and coding work on a major freelancing platform fell 21% relative to manual-intensive work, while the World Economic Forum expects AI and information processing to create 11 million jobs and displace 9 million worldwide by 2030. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it. We set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis. CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories.

### Do Your Candidates Only Freelance?

No — salaried jobs remain the largest pathway; freelancing and business ownership grew the most in relative terms. Among surveyed completers, salaried employment rose from 38.3% before training to 53.3% at the October 2026 survey, freelancing from 6.2% to 14.0% and business ownership from 2.8% to 10.6%.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program and shared after the needs assessment. Standard inclusions are a needs assessment, a customized curriculum, interactive workshops, progress evaluation and post-training support; certification exam preparation and talent sourcing are scoped separately where needed.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer and gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.

## An Independent Randomized Trial

A randomized trial by BIGD found that women offered a place in CodersTrust’s predecessor WSDFM program were 10.3 percentage points more likely to be employed. Their monthly income was an estimated 41% higher (about +$27 a month). For women who attended, income was 54% higher.

This trial evaluated WSDFM training for women in Dhaka, not JobReady@Work.
