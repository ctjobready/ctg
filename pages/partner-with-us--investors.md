## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

CodersTrust is building JobReady, an AI-native digital education and employment platform for emerging markets, on a track record of 130,000+ learners trained since 2014.

### Online Learning Sells Content, Not Earnings

Many online courses provide content without the work-entry support that turns skills into income: mentoring, certification and placement.

### AI Is Raising the Bar for Entry Work

Globally, jobs requiring AI skills grew 69% vs. 9% for all jobs, with a 62% wage premium.

Every trained young person who remains out of work is an opportunity employers and communities are not yet capturing.

It doesn’t have to be this way.

## Questions Investors Ask

Best Fit

Impact investors who back platform infrastructure for emerging-market workforces and can evaluate an early-stage rollout.

Not a Fit Yet

Investors who need a finished marketplace now: the employer marketplace and the gig-work (Earn) marketplace are in pre-launch.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly — modules are rolling out in stages. Live today: partner portals, marketing automation, CRM and AI chat. In pre-launch: the employer marketplace and the gig-work (Earn) marketplace, so placement also runs through CodersTrust’s placement team and marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are vulnerable to automation, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country, and agree each track’s AI content and work-entry plan with you before enrollment. We do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

In Her Power, a national program of DoICT, the ICT Division, CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; the national program trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas). We scale in phases: larger programs run up to 10 cohorts in parallel through certified local facilitators in existing labs and colleges. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we have not yet delivered.
