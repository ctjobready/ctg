## What Is CodersTrust?

CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

CodersTrust is building JobReady, a digital learning and earning platform for emerging markets.

### Is the JobReady Platform Live?

Partly. See What JobReady Does above for the live, pre-launch and planned capabilities.

### Will AI Eliminate the Jobs CodersTrust Trains For?

Some entry-level tasks in our tracks are vulnerable to automation, so we choose tracks from current employer demand, set per country, and agree each track’s AI content and work-entry plan with you before enrollment. We do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### Can You Deliver at National Scale?

In Her Power, a national program of DoICT, the ICT Division, CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines and trained 2,500 women directly; the national program trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas). We scale in phases: larger programs are designed to run up to 10 cohorts in parallel through certified local facilitators in existing labs and colleges. A 100,000+ national program is a design option we have not yet delivered.
