Context

## Why This Program

Her Power is a project of the ICT Division of the Government of Bangladesh. It aims to help women become entrepreneurs and self-employed through digital skills, using technology safely.

Within the national program, CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals, mentorship guidelines and trainer training, and trained a cohort of women directly.

## Challenges
- Keeping students engaged and motivated.
- Giving feedback without face-to-face contact.
- Instructors and learners who were new to online tools.

## What CodersTrust Did

CodersTrust’s role: Curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines; Training of Trainers; 2,400 women trained directly; 54,609 manuals and guidelines distributed.
- Extra support to help learners adopt digital tools.
- Interactive, collaborative sessions.
- Regular check-ins and Q&A to keep learners on track.

Results

## What Happened

Program Results

Program Record

Her Power (ICT Division / DoICT): 372 of 800 tracked women, from the 2,400 trained directly by CodersTrust, were in work (47%); 18.6% of those 800 were in work during the course.

### National Target: A Different Measure

The in-work figure above covers 800 tracked women from the 2,400 that CodersTrust trained directly. The national program’s target is separate: Her Power (DoICT, ICT Division): a national program to train 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas).
