A national program for women’s self-employment through digital skills, with CodersTrust as curriculum and training partner.

Context

## Why This Program

Her Power is a project of the ICT Division of the Government of Bangladesh. It aims to help women become entrepreneurs and self-employed through digital skills, using technology safely.

Within the national program, CodersTrust provided the curriculum, training manuals, mentorship guidelines and trainer training, and trained a cohort of women directly.

## Challenges
- Keeping students engaged and motivated.
- Giving feedback without face-to-face contact.
- Instructors and learners who were new to online tools.

## What CodersTrust Did

CodersTrust’s role: Curriculum, training manuals and mentorship guidelines; Training of Trainers; 2,500 women trained directly; 54,609 manuals and guidelines distributed.
- Extra support to help learners adopt digital tools.
- Interactive, collaborative sessions.
- Regular check-ins and Q&A to keep learners on track.

Results

## What Happened

Program Results

Program Record

Her Power (ICT Division / DoICT): 372 of 800 tracked women, from the 2,500 trained directly by CodersTrust, were in work (47%); 18.6% of those 800 were in work during the course.

### National Scale: A Different Measure

The in-work figure above covers 800 tracked women from the 2,500 that CodersTrust trained directly. The national program’s reach is counted separately: Her Power (DoICT, ICT Division): a national program that trained 25,125 women across 130 sub-districts (upazilas).

## A Learner Story

[image: Portrait of Maria Dewan]

Individual Story

Won a company logo design on Freelancer.com, then built a freelance portfolio.

Maria Dewan was a third-year Biology student when she first came across Her Power, where CodersTrust was a training partner. She enrolled in the Graphic Design course and, through her dedication and continuous enthusiasm for learning, was quickly initiated into the freelance marketplace. She showed her skills by winning a company logo design on Freelancer.com, and thus began her journey of earning on her own. She has continued her journey in the freelance space, with a growing portfolio, gradually winning more clients and higher-paying projects.
