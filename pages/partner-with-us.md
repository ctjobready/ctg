## From First Conversation to First Cohort

Four steps, then monthly reports. Who does what is clear from the start.
- ### Step 1: Discovery · Week 1

A conversation about your goals, priority groups and timeline.
- ### Step 2: Co-Design · Week 2

We shape tracks, cohorts, safeguarding and reporting with you; each track’s work-entry plan is approved before enrollment.
- ### Step 3: Agreement · Week 3

Scope, schedule and the pilot scorecard are agreed.
- ### Step 4: First Cohort · 6–8 Weeks

Grant-funded pilots: training starts in this window after signing. Monthly reports follow.

Timing: for grant-funded pilots. Public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.

## Monthly Reports and Quarterly Reviews

Monthly reports follow from the first cohort, so progress is visible while a program runs, not only at the end.

### What Your Monthly Report Covers
- Enrollment & Attendance
- Completion & Certification
- Placements & First Earnings
- Tracer Results (3/6/12 Months)
- Women and Priority-Group Shares
- Trainee Satisfaction

See the Sample Monthly Report on the YouthWIDE Page

### What You Decide at Each Review
- Review the Reports Together. You and CodersTrust hold a quarterly steering review of the monthly reports.
- Agree a Corrective Plan. A missed scorecard threshold triggers a corrective plan, agreed with you.
- Decide on Expansion. Expansion is the funder’s decision, taken on the scorecard results.
- Commission an Independent Evaluation. Independent evaluation is welcome, and we support it.

## Partner FAQs

Six questions partners ask before they start. Each audience page answers the rest.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

CodersTrust has run programs since 2014 in 15 countries and territories; our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, including a randomized trial of a 200-hour predecessor program; the Kosovo pilot adds program results. Each country version is localized with local partners, tracks and certifications. The course menu is set from local employer demand — typically digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, web development, digital accounting, Python programming and data analysis.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

Baseline at enrollment, tracer surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months, and documentary evidence of earnings where available; independent evaluation is welcome. Offer letters document job offers; payslips document employment earnings; platform logs or bank receipts document freelance earnings where available. Funders receive monthly reports on enrollment, completion, certification, placements and first earnings.

### Is There Independent Evidence?

Yes — a randomized controlled trial by BIGD (BRAC University) of CodersTrust’s WSDFM program studied women in Dhaka trained in 2020, on a 200-hour predecessor of today’s 100-hour track. It found that women offered a place were more likely to be employed than the control group. The trial surveyed 599 women at follow-up (report published May 2022). For context, a review of nine randomized trials of vocational training by World Bank economist David McKenzie (2017) found only three with significant employment impacts.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them. AI-ready curriculum: each track’s AI content and work-entry plan are agreed with you before enrollment; we do not yet publish a track-by-track breakdown.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. Each program design covers accessible selection, consent-based data collection, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response and referral pathways, with guardian consent and supervision for any participant under 18; funders receive privacy-appropriate data.

### What Happens When Funding Ends?

Where included in the program scope, what the program builds stays in place: certified local facilitators and localized curricula, and completers join CodersTrust’s alumni network after their three months of mentoring. TalentLEAP is designed to organize skills into career paths with progressive levels, so completers can keep advancing after a funded cohort ends.
