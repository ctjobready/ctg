CodersTrust is a workforce-development organization that trains and mentors educated, unemployed and underemployed youth in emerging markets for digital work and connects them to local, remote and global work.

## Your First 90 Days

Four steps from first conversation to first cohort. This timing is for grant-funded pilots; public procurement follows the agency’s own timeline.
- ### Step 1: Discovery (Week 1)

A conversation about your goals, priority groups and timeline.
- ### Step 2: Co-Design (Week 2)

We shape tracks, cohorts, safeguarding and reporting with you.
- ### Step 3: Agreement (Week 3)

Scope, schedule and the pilot scorecard are agreed.
- ### Step 4: Training Starts 6–8 Weeks After Signing

The first cohort begins training; monthly reports follow.

## Partner FAQs

Two questions every partner asks. Each route page answers the rest.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program; we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Supported youth cohorts include outreach and selection, training, certification exam preparation and the exam fee, three months of mentoring, placement support and 12 months of outcome tracking after each course. Devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where needed.
