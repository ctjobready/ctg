## Monthly Reports and Quarterly Reviews

Monthly reports follow from the first cohort, so progress is visible while a program runs, not only at the end.

### What Your Monthly Report Covers
- Enrollment and Attendance
- Completion and Certification
- Placements and First Earnings
- Follow-Up Survey Results (3/6/12 Months)
- Women and Priority-Group Shares
- Trainee Satisfaction

See the Sample Monthly Report on the YouthWIDE Page

### What You Decide at Each Review
- Review the Reports Together. You and CodersTrust hold a quarterly steering review of the monthly reports.
- Agree a Corrective Plan. A missed scorecard threshold triggers a corrective plan, agreed with you.
- Decide on Expansion. Expansion is the funder’s decision, taken on the scorecard results.
- Commission an Independent Evaluation. The decision is yours: independent evaluation is welcome, and we support it.

## Partner FAQs

Four questions every partner asks. Each route page answers the rest.

### Will This Work in Our Country?

That depends on local demand, which we test first. Our outcome evidence comes mainly from Bangladesh, and the Kosovo pilot adds program results from outside it; we set each country’s tracks with local partners, from local employer demand.

### How Do You Prove Outcomes?

For every cohort, as agreed in the program scope, we plan a baseline at enrollment, follow-up surveys at 3, 6 and 12 months and monthly reports. Documentary evidence of earnings, such as offer letters, payslips, platform logs or bank receipts, is collected where available. Independent evaluation is welcome.

### What Does It Cost?

Pricing is set per program, and we share an indicative budget in the discovery session. Standard inclusions are outreach and selection, blended training, an AI-ready curriculum, certification exam preparation (exam fee included), three months of mentoring, placement support, monthly reporting and outcome tracking for 12 months after each course; devices, stipends, connectivity and independent evaluation are budgeted separately where a program needs them.

### What About Safeguarding and Data Protection?

Safeguarding and data protection are specified for each program and agreed before enrollment. Each program design covers accessible selection, consent-based data collection, protected records, grievance handling, harassment and fraud response and referral pathways, with guardian consent and supervision for any participant under 18; funders receive privacy-appropriate data.
