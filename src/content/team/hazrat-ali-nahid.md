Md. Hazrat Ali Nahid is Team Lead of the Education Department at CodersTrust Bangladesh, where he also serves as Head of Digital Accounting and Mentor Co-Ordinator in Team Education. He is a Cloud Accounting Expert and Tax Consultant.

He holds a Master's in Accounting and is a registered PTIN tax preparer in the USA. With over 15 years of experience in bookkeeping, payroll, VAT, GST, and tax filing, he is proficient in accounting software including QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, Zoho Books, Sage 50, FreshBooks, QuickBooks Payroll, and Gusto Payroll.

He has trained over 30,000 students globally, and his expertise spans the US, UK, Australia, and Canada.
