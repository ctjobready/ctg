Ferdinand Kjærulff, a former Danish Army captain, entrepreneur, and businessman, founded CodersTrust in 2014 together with Aziz Ahmad. He set out to address a particular gap: while online labor markets and learning platforms exist, there was no financial system to fund the education of tomorrow's online workforce.

His aim was that, by capturing the return on investment from the online labor market, CodersTrust could make education universally accessible and an appealing investment for the capital market. He believed that building this inclusive financial system for bright students around the world could have a powerful impact on the poorest people in the world.

Ferdinand passed away in February 2023 after fighting cancer. CodersTrust honors his visionary spirit by continuing to provide the youth of the world with skill-based digital education with globally recognized industry certifications.
