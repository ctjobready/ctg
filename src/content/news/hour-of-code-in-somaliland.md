In late 2015, CodersTrust’s work in Somaliland included an Hour of Code event for students, and an ICT program at Tima-ade University. Hour of Code is a worldwide one-hour introduction to coding.

That month CodersTrust invited people in Bangladesh, India, Kenya and Somaliland to take part in the Hour of Code, as its [post of December 21, 2015](https://coderstrust.tumblr.com/post/135629572265/coderstrust-hour-of-code-in-a-nutshell-just-over) reported. The photograph shows a group in front of a Tima-ade University banner.

A [Somali television news item of November 29, 2015](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-8MZdwf13HM), from Gabiley, names Coders Trust, the university (spelled “Timacade”) and its ICT program.
