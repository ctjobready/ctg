In December 2015, CodersTrust held an Hour of Code event for students in Somaliland, with Tima-ade University. Hour of Code is a worldwide one-hour introduction to coding.

That month CodersTrust invited people in Bangladesh, India, Kenya and Somaliland to take part, as its [post of December 21, 2015](https://coderstrust.tumblr.com/post/135629572265/coderstrust-hour-of-code-in-a-nutshell-just-over) reported. The photograph shows participants in front of the university’s banner.
