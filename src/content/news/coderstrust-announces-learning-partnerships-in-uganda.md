In September 2020, CodersTrust Global announced learning partnerships in Uganda with Maarifasasa, Makerere University Business School and U-Touch.

The [release of September 10, 2020](https://web.archive.org/web/20210127142038/https://academy.coderstrust.com/pages/coderstrust-secures-education-partnerships-in-uganda-benin-somaliland-and-egypt-to-expand) describes partnerships that combine online and blended classroom learning with local mentors. It also announced partnerships in Benin, with SOS Children’s Villages, in Egypt, with CrossWorkers, and in Somaliland. A [Makerere University Business School page on its ICT unit](https://mubs.ac.ug/unit/mubs-ict-centre/) says the school partners with CodersTrust Global Academy to offer the skills most in demand in freelancing, and Maarifasasa published a [video about the partnership](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eLjk9BvbOBo).
