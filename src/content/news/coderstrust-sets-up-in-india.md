CodersTrust began operating in India in 2015 and had an office in Gurugram in 2016.

In October 2015, Tech in Asia wrote, in a report [reprinted by NextBillion](https://nextbillion.net/news/this-microfinance-startup-teaches-students-in-bangladesh-and-africa-how-to-code/), that CodersTrust had recently launched initiatives in India and parts of Africa, while most of its work was in Bangladesh. On June 29, 2016, [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/jobs/denmark-company-coderstrust-looks-to-move-to-pune/articleshow/52967471.cms) reported that the company had set up operations in India and was moving to Pune to make it its hub for the region. On August 7, 2016, [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/home/sunday-times/Cook-to-coder-How-low-income-youth-are-writing-a-better-future/articleshow/53575603.cms) featured the head of CodersTrust’s India operations in a story about how low-income youth are learning to code.
