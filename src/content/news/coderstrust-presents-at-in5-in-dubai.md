In April 2014, CodersTrust’s leaders spoke at a start-up event at In5, Dubai Internet City, and met investors and potential partners in the Gulf.

The evening, on how to unite and grow the start-up ecosystem in the United Arab Emirates, was held at In5, the innovation hub at Dubai Internet City. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/skype-founder-morten-lund-returns-to-uae-with-new-venture-1.464863) and [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/skype-co-founder-seeks-local-partners-in-new-venture-1.1327190) reported on CodersTrust’s visit at the time, and CodersTrust’s own [event page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140701074025/http://coderstrust.com/dubai-event) describes the evening.
