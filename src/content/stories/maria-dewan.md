Her Power is a project of Bangladesh’s ICT Division, with training delivered in part by CodersTrust.

Maria Dewan was a third-year Biology student when she first came across the Her Power project implemented by CodersTrust. She enrolled in the Graphic Design course and, through her dedication and continuous enthusiasm for learning, was quickly initiated into the freelance marketplace. She showed her skills by winning a company logo design on Freelancer.com, and thus began her journey of earning on her own.

She has continued her journey in the freelance space, with a growing portfolio, gradually winning more clients and higher-paying projects.
