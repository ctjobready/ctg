For the students of the STRONG-IT project, freelancing not only brought hope, but also paved the way to career success.

Arfias Islam was a student of Graphic Design and was fortunate enough to land his first Fiverr project, worth $10, during the course of his studies. Although the project fee was relatively small, the student approached the task with great enthusiasm and dedication, and received $12 as a token of appreciation. This initial success motivated the student to continue pursuing freelance projects with even more determination. He continued to work hard and build his portfolio, gradually winning more clients and higher-paying projects.

His story doesn't end there. His hard work and dedication eventually paid off when he was offered a full-time job at a reputable design company. His experience as a freelancer gave him a unique perspective and valuable skills that he was able to bring to his new role.

In the end, what started as a small project worth just $10 turned into a life-changing opportunity for him.
