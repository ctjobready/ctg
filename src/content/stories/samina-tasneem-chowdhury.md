Samina Tasneem Chowdhury, a trainee from the first batch of the Women's Skill Development for Freelancing Marketplace (WSDFM) project, was a bright and ambitious girl from a middle-income family in Bangladesh.

Despite facing challenges as a female, Tasneem was determined to continue her education. During the training period, she began offering her services as a graphic designer on the freelance marketplace Fiverr.

Her dedication during the course and hard work paid off, and soon she became a successful freelancer with a thriving career. She has completed 90+ projects on Fiverr so far.

She is one of the many students from the WSDFM project. Today, she is an inspiration to the young people in her community. She continues to work as a freelancer, providing a better life for herself and her family, and offering advice and encouragement to others.
