CodersTrust offers Post-Graduate Diploma courses of National University and serves as the Official Training Partner for Digital Career Skills and Credentials. CodersTrust's College Code with National University is 6634.

The diploma courses are designed for highly relevant, future-fit job roles, so that learners gain the technical knowledge to improve their domain in technical and IT skills. The program offers four high-demand courses with experienced mentors who have long been involved in upskilling learners in technology.

## What the program offers

- Specially tailored courses designed by industry experts at National University and delivered by CodersTrust mentors
- Job placement opportunity at the end of semesters
- Career counseling seminars in each semester
- One-to-one career counseling option
- One free course of your choice from CodersTrust's 120+ virtual courses

## Courses

- Post-Graduate Diploma in Digital Marketing: 40 credit hours, 10 subjects, instructor-led
- Post-Graduate Diploma in Information & Communication Technology: 40 credit hours, 10 subjects, instructor-led
- Upcoming: Cybersecurity and Data Analytics

## How to apply

There are three application routes: online, offline, and by courier.

**Online.** Fill out the application form online, then email your academic certificates. After the certificates are verified online, you will be told the date and schedule of the final selection interview.

**Offline.** Fill out the form in person at the office and submit your academic certificates there for verification. After verification you will be notified of the final selection interview and its date.

**Courier.** Complete the form online before dispatching it by courier, or obtain forms directly from CodersTrust offices. Academic certificates are submitted by courier. After verification you will be informed of the final selection interview and its schedule.

## Eligibility and documents

- Graduate from any reputed university
- Cumulative GPA of 7 or above from SSC through Honors
- Completed online or offline application
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- Academic certificates: SSC, HSC, and graduate degree
