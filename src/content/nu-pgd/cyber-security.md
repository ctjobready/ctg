The Postgraduate Diploma in Cyber Security is designed to help students understand how to protect computer systems, networks and digital information from cyber threats and online attacks. The programme covers cyber criminology, cryptography and data security, network and communication security, cyber security laws and policies, cybercrime investigation and digital forensics. Students also explore digital privacy, cyber risk management, cyber terrorism and financial cybercrime. The final project or internship allows students to apply their knowledge to real-world situations and gain practical experience in a relevant organization.

## Course objectives

- Understand the fundamental concepts, classifications, causes, and impacts of cybercrime in contemporary society
- Develop foundational knowledge of cryptography, data security, information protection, and digital privacy
- Understand network and communication security principles, including common vulnerabilities, threats, and protective measures
- Examine cyber security laws, policies, regulations, and strategies relevant to Bangladesh and international contexts
- Understand the principles and procedures of cybercrime investigation, digital forensics, and digital evidence management
- Identify cyber security threats, assess vulnerabilities, and understand appropriate prevention, mitigation, and incident response measures
- Apply cyber security risk management concepts to identify, evaluate, document, and address organizational risks
- Analyze cyber economic crimes, financial cybercrime patterns, and approaches to crime prevention and management
- Understand threats associated with social media, online platforms, cyber terrorism, and the misuse of digital technologies
- Apply academic knowledge and analytical skills to cyber security-related problems through a capstone project or internship

## What you'll learn

- Explain major forms of cybercrime, cyber security threats, and their potential effects on individuals, organizations, and society
- Demonstrate foundational understanding of cryptography, data protection, network security, and digital privacy principles
- Identify common cyber threats, system vulnerabilities, and security risks, and explain appropriate preventive or remedial measures
- Explain the principles of cybercrime investigation, digital forensics, evidence preservation, and incident response
- Analyze cyber security risks, relevant legal and policy considerations, and strategies for protecting digital systems and information
- Apply critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and problem-solving skills to cyber security related case studies, projects, or internship assignments
