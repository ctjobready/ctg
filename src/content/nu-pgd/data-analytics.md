The Postgraduate Diploma in Data Analytics introduces students to the essential concepts, techniques, and tools used to transform raw data into meaningful insights. Through practical exercises, case studies, and project-based learning, students develop the ability to work with datasets, interpret analytical findings, and communicate insights effectively. The program aims to build technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills that can be applied in business, finance, marketing, development organizations, research, and other professional fields.

## Course objectives

- Understand the fundamental concepts and applications of Data Analytics
- Collect, organize, clean, and prepare data for analysis
- Apply spreadsheet functions and analytical techniques to interpret datasets
- Understand basic statistical concepts and their applications in data analysis
- Use data visualization techniques to communicate findings clearly
- Develop dashboards, charts, and analytical reports to support decision-making
- Understand database concepts and retrieve relevant information from datasets
- Identify trends, patterns, and relationships in data
- Apply analytical thinking to practical business and organizational problems
- Present data-driven insights and recommendations to relevant stakeholders

## What you'll learn

- Understand the data analytics lifecycle, from data collection to interpretation and reporting
- Organize, clean, validate, and prepare datasets for analysis
- Use spreadsheet tools and formulas to perform data calculations and analysis
- Apply basic statistical methods to identify patterns and trends
- Understand database fundamentals and data retrieval concepts
- Create charts, dashboards, and visual reports to communicate analytical findings
- Interpret data to support business planning and operational decisions
- Identify relevant performance indicators and evaluate organizational performance
- Communicate insights through clear reports, presentations, and visualizations
- Apply analytical and problem-solving skills to practical projects and case studies
- Build a foundation for further learning in business intelligence and advanced analytics
- Develop professional, managerial, and communication skills for data-related roles
