## Your Role and Responsibilities

To join our vibrant PR and Communications team, we are looking for an Associate Manager or Senior Associate that is extremely driven and skilled. This position presents a stimulating chance to support the creation and implementation of strategic communication plans that improve our brand’s reputation and encourage interaction with key important stakeholders.

### Public Relations (PR)

- Create and carry out a plan for marketing communications that includes a strategy, objectives, financial plan and strategies.
- Develop connections with different stakeholders; B2B and B2G.
- Obtain prominent positions in broadcast, print and online media.
- Assemble media kits, press releases and other communication resources.

### Communications

- Create and put into action a thorough communication plan that is in line with the objectives of the company.
- Provide engaging material for a range of platforms, including social media, blogs, press releases and articles.
- Make sure the messaging is the same across all of the platforms.
- Work together with internal teams to efficiently distribute information.

### Content Development

- Oversee the creation of excellent instructional materials such as infographics, webinars, videos, case studies and whitepapers that highlight our knowledge and solutions.
- Collaborate closely with specialists in the field to pinpoint content opportunities and guarantee precision and pertinence.
- Make content more engaging for readers and search engines (SEO) by utilizing analytics and statistics to inform ongoing enhancements.

### Demand Generation

- Assist with attempts to generate demand by spearheading nurturing campaigns, email marketing and strategic content distribution.
- Work together to create effective messaging and assets for lead generation and conversion with the sales and marketing teams.
- Monitor and evaluate campaign performance indicators to maximize efficacy.
- Monitor and analyze campaign performance metrics.

### Event Management

- Organize, plan and carry out in-person and virtual events, such as conferences, workshops, networking events and webinars.
- Oversee all facets of the event’s planning, such as choosing the location, dealing with vendors, scheduling speakers and interacting with attendees.
- Make sure everything runs smoothly, from pre-event marketing to on-site assistance to post-event follow-up.
- Represent the organization at industry events and conferences.

### Master of Ceremony (MC)

- Serve as a confident and engaging master of ceremonies for company events, ensuring a professional and memorable experience for attendees.
- Organize the event’s flow, introduce speakers and involve the audience.
- Preserve a polished and lively environment.
- Serve as a brand ambassador by emulating the company’s attitude and ideals both on stage and when interacting with attendees.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- A bachelor’s degree in journalism, marketing, public relations, communications or a similar discipline.
- 15 years of experience, ideally in the ed-tech sector (not mandatory), in PR, corporate communications, content marketing or related jobs.
- Strong communication abilities both in writing and speaking, along with an interest in the education field and storytelling.
- Strong multitasking and prioritization abilities in a fast-paced setting, coupled with project management expertise.
- Proficiency with Google Workspace, the Microsoft Office Suite and PR tools and software.
- Familiarity with email marketing platforms, social media management tools and content management systems.
- Proven capacity to influence stakeholders at all levels and collaborate across teams.
- Positivity, adaptability, flexibility and a desire to progress are all present.
- Speaking in front of an audience or having prior master of ceremonies experience are advantages.
