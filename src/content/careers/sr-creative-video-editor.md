## Your Role and Responsibilities

We are seeking a Senior Video Editor to join our creative team and produce engaging and high-quality video content for our clients and audiences. The following list of key responsibility areas (KRAs) and responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

- Demonstrate an excellent sense of storytelling, pacing and visual aesthetics. Utilize creative skills to enhance project narratives and ensure engaging content delivery.
- Manage the post-production process, including editing, color grading, sound design and motion graphics.
- Edit raw footage into polished and engaging videos that match our vision and objectives.
- Add sound effects, music, graphics and other elements to enhance the video quality and impact.
- Oversee multiple projects from start to finish, managing timelines, resources and deliverables.
- Ensure that all video content is delivered on time and meets the highest standards of quality as per company brand guidelines.
- Collaborate with videographers, producers, directors and other creative professionals to deliver the best possible results.
- Provide feedback and creative guidance to junior video editors and assist them with their development.
- Stay updated with the latest trends and best practices in video editing and production.
- Manage and maintain video editing equipment and software, troubleshooting technical issues as they arise.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- 10+ years of proven experience as a video editor, preferably in a digital media or agency environment.
- Bachelor’s degree in film, video production or a related field preferred.
- Proficient in using video editing software, such as Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve.
- Extensive knowledge of color grading, sound design, motion graphics, Adobe Audition, Premiere Pro audio processing tools and plugins, Audacity, OBS, Steelseries Sonar.
- Good understanding and expertise of audio capture and screen-recording quality optimizations using tools like OBS, particularly noise removal, suppression and noise-gate settings, plus the post-processing noise removal and voice quality improvements in Premiere.
- Excellent sense of storytelling, pacing and visual aesthetics. Ability to work creatively within project constraints and deliver high-quality content.
- Portfolio of previous video editing work, demonstrating creativity, technical skills and storytelling ability.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a team environment.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Strong understanding of storytelling principles and visual storytelling techniques.
- Proficiency with Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop editing.
- Expertise in 3D Animation design.
