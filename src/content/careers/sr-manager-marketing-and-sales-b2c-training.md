## Your Role and Responsibilities

You’ll be responsible for developing and implementing marketing and sales strategies that drive business growth and enhance the company’s brand reputation. You will oversee all aspects of the marketing and sales function, including market research, product development, pricing, promotions, advertising and customer engagement.

The following list of typical responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

- Develop and implement strategic marketing and sales plans that align with the company’s business objectives.
- Conduct market research to identify customer needs, preferences and buying behavior and use insights to inform product development and pricing strategies.
- Develop and manage the marketing budget, ensuring that all initiatives are cost-effective and aligned with sales targets.
- Lead the development and execution of marketing campaigns, including digital marketing, social media, email marketing and content marketing.
- Develop sales plans and strategies that drive customer acquisition and retention, working closely with the sales team to identify target markets, sales channels and pricing models.
- Monitor and analyze market trends, competitor activity and customer feedback to identify opportunities and areas of improvement.
- Develop and maintain relationships with key stakeholders, including customers, partners, vendors and industry influencers.
- Manage and mentor the marketing and sales team members, providing guidance, training and feedback as needed.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product development, customer support and finance, to ensure consistent messaging and customer experience across all touchpoints.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- 15+ years of experience in marketing and sales, preferably in a leadership role.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Marketing or a related field
- Proven track record of developing and executing successful marketing and sales strategies that drive sales growth.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Excellent leadership, interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to effectively collaborate with internal and external stakeholders.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to use data to inform decision-making.
- Experience managing budgets, timelines and resources for complex projects.
- Familiarity with customer relationship management software, marketing automation tools and other marketing and sales technologies.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, with a strong focus on results and customer satisfaction.
