## Your Role and Responsibilities

The following list of key responsibility areas (KRAs) and responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

- Idea Creation, Work with the creative team to generate innovative and visually compelling ideas for storytelling, shot composition and overall visual style
- Storyboarding, Contribute to the development of storyboards, collaborating with the creative team to plan shot sequences and visualize the narrative
- Cinematography, Capture high-quality, cinematic footage that aligns with the creative vision of each project
- Camera Operation, Operate a variety of cameras and equipment, ensuring technical proficiency and optimal shot composition
- Lighting, Design and set up lighting schemes that enhance the visual impact of each scene while maintaining consistency with the overall aesthetic
- Collaboration, Work closely with the team members to understand and contribute to the overall creative vision of the project
- Problem Solving, Proactively identify and address technical and creative challenges to ensure smooth production processes
- Equipment Management, Oversee and maintain camera equipment, ensuring it is in optimal condition for each shoot
- Research and Development, Stay updated on industry trends, new technologies and cinematography techniques to continually enhance skills and contribute fresh ideas to projects
- Video Editing Mastery, Skillfully transform raw footage into a polished final product using editing software, ensuring seamless visuals, audio coherence and alignment with project objectives
- Post-Production Collaboration, Work closely with the editing team to ensure the seamless transition from shooting to post-production while preserving the intended creative vision

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Proven experience as a Cinematographer in digital content production.
- Strong portfolio showcasing a diverse range of cinematography work.
- Proficiency in operating a variety of cameras, lenses and other cinematography equipment.
- Solid understanding of lighting techniques and the ability to adapt to different lighting conditions.
- Experience in storyboarding and visual narrative planning.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, creative environment and meet tight deadlines.
- Familiarity with post-production processes and collaboration with the editing team.
- Knowledge of the latest trends and technologies in cinematography.
- Degree in Cinematography, Film Production or a related field is preferred.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- A Master’s degree or higher in Cinematography, Film Production or a related field is preferred. Advanced education demonstrates a commitment to continuous learning and a deeper understanding of the craft
