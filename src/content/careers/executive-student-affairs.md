## Your Role and Responsibilities

We’re seeking an Executive, Student Operation who can support the smooth running of our student services and programs. You will work closely with the Student Operation Lead and other team members to coordinate student activities, events and communication. You will also assist in managing student records, data and feedback. This role requires excellent organizational, interpersonal and communication skills, as well as a passion for student success and engagement.

The following list of typical responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can ensure high-quality results.

- Overseeing and managing student affairs issues such as class absences, postponed, refund appeals, relevant issues, coordinating and communicating with Mentors for student support.
- Coordinating workshops, seminar, training sessions and retreats for students and facilitates all facets of on-campus student orientation and relevant activities.
- Overseeing marketing, administration and logistics for student operation and official events.
- Counseling and conferring with students for providing class procedures, queries, technical guidance and problem resolution.
- Presentations on “Student affairs and Operation team” and their activity for new batches orientation.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor degree in any discipline
- 2 year(s) of experience in relevant field

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Organizing and interpersonal skills and positive attitude, strong computer skills, including Microsoft suite and customer relationship management experience preferred.
- Good presentation skill is a must.
