## Your Role and Responsibilities

The following list of key responsibility areas (KRAs) and responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

### Course Development

- Collaborating with Instructors and course production team and assisting Sr. Managers in the Course Development team – design, develop and maintain CBT&A-based (Competency-based Training and Assessment), skill-oriented, effective and engaging digital learning content for both global and local learners.
- Manage development and production of courses focused on various Digital Economy competencies that ensure learning outcomes, including competency models, course outlines, lesson plans, learning resources, video tutorials, assessments, etc.
- Acquire and coordinate instructors and mentors to develop various courses.
- Communicate and conduct training and mentoring sessions for mentors and instructors.
- Design and develop PowerPoint presentations for courses.

### Production Coordination

- Manage the end-to-end production process of course materials, including video lectures, presentations, quizzes and other multimedia assets.
- Coordinate closely with multimedia specialists, graphic designers and content creators to ensure the timely delivery of high-quality course materials.
- Monitor production schedules and budgets, proactively addressing any challenges or delays to ensure projects stay on track.
- Develop engaging and persuasive promotional scripts, presentations and storyboarding for video productions, outlining shot sequences and visual.
- Manage the launch process of courses and video content to the designated portals and manage a comprehensive archival system for all media assets, including raw footage, edited videos and promotional materials.
- Maintain detailed documentation of course development processes, including project plans, timelines and resource allocations.
- Generate comprehensive reports on course development progress, production metrics and quality assurance findings, providing insights to inform decision-making.
- Communicate project updates, milestones and challenges to relevant stakeholders in a clear and timely manner.

### Course Outcomes Assurance

- Implement robust quality control and assurance processes to ensure that course content meets established standards for accuracy, consistency and effectiveness.
- Conduct thorough reviews of course materials, providing constructive feedback and guidance to enhance content quality and alignment with learning objectives.
- Monitor and follow up on learner progress of cohorts to determine refinements necessary in the approach and model.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor’s degree in science or a related field from a reputed university.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Bangla and English, with the ability to communicate effectively.
- Ability to assist the team to successfully coordinate complex projects and delivering high-quality educational content.
- Strong organizational skills and meticulous attention to detail.
- Proficiency in project management tools and software applications.
- Creative and proficient in Adobe Photoshop and Microsoft PowerPoint.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- In-depth knowledge of the training industry of Bangladesh.
- Experience with e-learning platforms and digital training solutions.
- Familiarity with learning management systems (LMS) and other training technologies.
