## Your Role and Responsibilities

### Teaching/Training Skills

- Effective Communication: Clear communication to explain complex concepts.
- Engaging Teaching Methods: Creating interactive learning experiences through lectures, demonstrations and hands-on labs.
- Curriculum Development: Structuring curriculum, lesson plans and assessments.
- Adaptability: Tailoring teaching approaches to diverse student backgrounds.
- Mentoring Skills: Guiding students with patience and empathy.

### Industry Certifications

- CISSP: Certified Information Systems Security Professional.
- CEH: Certified Ethical Hacker.
- CompTIA Security+
- CISM: Certified Information Security Manager.
- CISA: Certified Information Systems Auditor.
- GSEC: GIAC Security Essentials.
- OSCP: Offensive Security Certified Professional.

### Professional Experience

- Hands-On Experience: Proficiency with cybersecurity concepts, tools and techniques.
- Previous Roles: Valuable background in positions like cybersecurity analyst, engineer or consultant.
- Domain Expertise: Familiarity with network security, application security, cloud security, cryptography and incident response.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor’s Degree: Typically required in fields like computer science, cybersecurity or information technology.
- 3 to 5 years of experience in relevant fields.
- Advanced Degrees: Master’s or Ph.D. can enhance qualifications, particularly for teaching at higher education institutions.
