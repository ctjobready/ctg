## Your Role and Responsibilities

As the Sr. Manager, Business Development and Sales, you will be responsible for leading the development of strategic partnerships and customized business-to-government solutions, business-to-business, business-to-consumer, using your extensive expertise in company development. With a deep understanding of government procurement processes, you will drive international market expansion, increase sales and promote comprehensive job readiness solutions that empower workers to excel and advance in their careers.

### Strategic Leadership and Planning

- Formulate and execute comprehensive global marketing strategies that align with the company’s objectives.
- Develop detailed marketing and sales plans to achieve sales goals and expand the market share of JobReady@Work products.
- Conduct market trend analysis, competitive landscape assessment and customer insights to guide strategic planning.
- Perform market research to identify competitive opportunities.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to synchronize sales and marketing efforts with company-wide objectives and product roadmap.

### Group Leadership

- Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration and excellence within a team of marketing and sales professionals.
- Provide mentorship, guidance and support to team members to enhance their skills, productivity and professional growth.
- Set clear objectives, KPIs and performance benchmarks, monitoring progress to ensure alignment with company goals.

### Sales Enablement

- Design and implement initiatives that strengthen the sales team, enhancing sales through resources, tools and training.
- Develop sales materials and presentations.
- Collaborate with product development and management teams to align product offerings with market demands.
- Craft value propositions, marketing campaigns and pricing strategies to effectively position JobReady@Work solutions in the market.

### Account Management

- Manage all facets of the sales cycle, including contract negotiations, prospecting and post-sale support.
- Identify and execute successful sales opportunities.
- Achieve monthly and annual sales targets.

### Global Marketing Team Management

- Lead and direct a diverse, global marketing team.
- Set and evaluate clear performance goals for team members.
- Promote a collaborative and innovative work environment, encouraging open communication and idea exchange.

### Budget Management

- Develop and oversee marketing budgets to ensure resource efficiency.
- Allocate resources strategically for maximum impact on marketing initiatives.
- Monitor expenditures to optimize budget use.

### Stakeholder Engagement

- Cultivate strong relationships with internal stakeholders, including sales, product and finance teams.
- Work with regional marketing teams to tailor global strategies to local markets.
- Represent the marketing department in leadership meetings, providing insights and updates.

### Customer Acquisition and Retention

- Lead customer acquisition efforts through strategic partnerships, targeted marketing campaigns and lead generation.
- Develop and maintain relationships with key influencers, decision-makers and clients to foster product adoption and retention.
- Implement customer retention strategies to enhance satisfaction and loyalty.

### Performance Analysis and Reporting

- Establish critical performance metrics to gauge marketing and sales initiative effectiveness and promote ongoing improvement.
- Analyze sales data, customer feedback and market trends to uncover optimization and innovation opportunities.
- Produce regular reports and presentations to communicate sales performance and market insights to senior leadership.

### Business Development

- Spearheaded the identification and pursuit of government sector opportunities aligned with organizational strategic goals.
- Engage efficiently with high-level government officials.
- Build and sustain relationships with key government agency stakeholders.
- Negotiate and draft agreements for B2G partnerships, contracts and procurement opportunities, facilitating business development endeavors.
- Navigate the intricacies of government procurement processes, enabling them to identify and pursue lucrative business opportunities within the public sector.
- Identifying and mitigating risks associated with B2G engagements, safeguarding business interests and minimizing potential liabilities.

### Relationship Management

- Maintain lasting relationships with government clients, positioning the company as a trusted advisor and partner.
- Ensure client needs are met and expectations exceeded as the primary point of contact.

### Market Analysis and Strategy

- Conduct market research to identify trends, opportunities and competitive threats in the government sector.
- Develop and refine go-to-market strategies for government products based on market demands and trends.
- Stay informed about policy and regulatory changes affecting government procurement.

### Client Identification and Research

- Perform market research to identify potential government sector clients and understand their needs.
- Build trust with decision-makers and government entities.

### Proposal Development and Negotiation

- Craft compelling proposals for government clients.
- Negotiate contract terms and maintain effective stakeholder communication.
- Form strategic partnerships to drive business growth.

### RFP Management

- Meticulously reviews RFPs and EOIs to ensure that proposed solutions or expressions of interest comply with applicable laws, regulations and contractual requirements.
- Identify potential legal risks associated with RFPs and EOIs, such as liability exposure or contractual disputes and develop strategies to mitigate these risks.
- Oversee the RFP submission process, ensuring adherence to deadlines and requirements.
- Create detailed responses to RFPs, collaborating with cross-functional teams for accurate and complete proposals.
- When responding to RFPs or EOIs, ensure that terms and conditions align with the requirements set forth in the solicitation documents.
- Conduct reviews and quality checks to ensure proposal clarity and compliance.

### Sales Pipeline Management

- Oversee the development and maintenance of the sales pipeline, ensuring a consistent flow of qualified leads and opportunities.
- Implement strategies to nurture leads through the sales process, from initial contact to conversion, optimizing for efficiency and effectiveness.
- Utilize data analytics to track and analyze pipeline metrics, identifying areas for improvement and optimizing sales performance.
- Collaborate with sales teams to prioritize leads, allocate resources effectively and drive towards achieving sales targets and goals.
- Regular monitoring of project progress is essential to stay on track. Updates are provided to both internal stakeholders and the client to maintain transparency and manage expectations.

### B2G Business Development and Sales

- Negotiate and draft agreements for B2G partnerships, contracts and procurement opportunities, facilitating business development endeavors.
- Track record of accomplishment in company development for the B2G industry with an emphasis on workforce development programs.
- Identifying and mitigating risks associated with B2G engagements, safeguarding business interests and minimizing potential liabilities.

### Identifying and Understanding Government Needs

- This includes researching to comprehend the specific requirements and challenges faced by various government agencies.

### Proposal Development

- Crafting detailed proposals in response to government requests for proposals (RFPs), which requires a deep understanding of the procurement process.

### Establishing Relationships

- Building and maintaining strong relationships with government officials and decision-makers.

### Market Research

- Conducting thorough market research to align business offerings with government objectives and policies.

### Contract Management

- Overseeing the bidding process, contract negotiations and management of awarded government contracts.

### B2C Marketing and Sales

- Develop and execute targeted marketing campaigns to reach individual job seekers.
- Manage social media presence and online advertising campaigns.
- Develop content marketing strategies to attract and engage potential customers.
- Oversee lead generation and nurturing efforts for individual sales.
- Design and implement sales funnels to convert leads into paying customers.
- Manage pricing strategies for B2C products and services.
- Partner with the B2B sales team to identify and capitalize on cross-selling opportunities.

### JobReady@Campus (JRC) Partnerships with Universities, Colleges and Polytechnics

- Develop strategies to roll out JRC.
- Conduct workshops, etc. to generate leads.
- Develop and maintain strong relationships with key personnel at partner institutions.
- Negotiate and finalize partnership agreements.
- Oversee the implementation of JobReady@Campus programs at partner institutions.
- Track and measure the success of JRC programs, including student participation rates and job placement outcomes.
- Identify and develop new partnership opportunities with educational institutions.

### B2B – JobReady@Work (JRW) Business Development and Sales

- Develop and execute a comprehensive sales strategy for JobReady@Work solutions targeted towards businesses.
- Identify and qualify potential B2B leads.
- Manage the sales cycle for B2B opportunities, including prospecting, qualification, presentations, negotiations and closing deals.
- Develop and deliver customized proposals for B2B clients.
- Foster long-term relationships with key decision-makers at client companies.

### Overall Management of Ctg Branch

- Provide strategic direction and leadership for the Ctg branch team.
- Oversee all aspects of branch operations, including sales, marketing, customer service, accounts and administration.
- Set performance goals and objectives for the branch team.
- Monitor and track branch performance against key metrics.
- Identify and implement strategies to improve branch efficiency and effectiveness.

### Administrative Tasks

- Manage and prioritize workload effectively.
- Schedule appointments and meetings.
- Prepare presentations and reports.
- Maintain accurate records and documentation.
- Travel to various locations and cities within the country to meet with clients and partners (if applicable).

### Skills Required

- Ability to effectively influence and guide cross-functional teams.
- In-depth knowledge of trends, challenges and opportunities within the education technology sector.
- The capacity to develop long-term visions and strategies.
- Competence in identifying issues and implementing solutions.
- Proficient in articulating ideas and engaging with stakeholders.
- Skilled in presenting information clearly and persuasively.
- Utilizing data analytics to inform strategic choices.
- Expertise in online marketing strategies and tools.
- Knowledgeable in using analytics and customer relationship management software.
- Understanding and appreciating cultural diversity and global market dynamics.
- Sensitivity to cultural differences and the ability to adapt to diverse environments.
