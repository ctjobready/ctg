## Your Role and Responsibilities

The CRO will unify all revenue-generating departments, including global sales, marketing, partnerships and customer success, under a cohesive strategy. This role will focus on unlocking new revenue streams, optimizing operations and ensuring financial performance in a competitive EdTech landscape.

The ideal candidate combines a visionary mindset with practical execution skills, leveraging deep expertise in revenue generation, data-driven decision-making and leadership of high-performing teams.

### Strategic Revenue Leadership

- Revenue Growth Strategy: Develop and implement a unified revenue growth strategy aligned with CodersTrust’s business goals and mission.
- Market Penetration: Identify high-potential markets and design entry strategies that maximize revenue growth and learner impact.
- Innovation in Revenue Streams: Explore and monetize innovative offerings, such as subscription models, corporate training packages and licensing agreements.
- Scalable Solutions: Build scalable revenue systems to support CodersTrust’s growth in diverse international markets.
- Comprehensive Strategy: Implement a comprehensive revenue generating strategy for B2G, B2C and overseas markets.
- Leadership Collaboration: Collaborate with the leadership team to set revenue objectives, budgets and KPIs.
- Department Integration: Ensure seamless integration and synchronization of revenue-related departments, such as sales, marketing and business development.
- Fundraising: The individual will secure money for the company from several sources, including venture capital, social impact investors, banks and NBFIs.

### Sales and Market Strategies

- B2G Strategies: Lead and oversee B2G sales strategies to secure government contracts and collaborations.
- Government Relationships: Establish relationships with government stakeholders and understand procurement processes and compliance requirements.
- Tenders and Opportunities: Identify and respond to government tenders, RFPs and strategic opportunities.
- Consumer Channels: Optimize direct-to-consumer sales channels for better customer acquisition and retention.
- Data Analytics: Use data analytics to improve consumer insights, pricing tactics and customer experiences.
- Marketing Collaboration: Collaborate with the marketing team to create and implement initiatives that increase customer demand and brand loyalty.

### International Collaboration (USA Focus)

- US Partnerships: Manage strategic partnerships with organizations, distributors and stakeholders in the USA.
- Market Entry: Determine market entrance methods, regulatory needs and cultural factors for the US market.
- Global Representation: Represent the organization at global trade exhibits, networking events and negotiations.

### Global Sales and Revenue Optimization

- Sales Leadership: Lead global sales teams to achieve ambitious revenue targets through effective pipeline management and robust forecasting.
- Enterprise Sales Strategy: Drive enterprise and institutional sales by targeting corporate clients, governments and academic institutions.
- Deal Structuring: Oversee high-stakes negotiations to secure large-scale contracts and strategic partnerships.
- Revenue Accountability: Monitor and manage KPIs such as customer acquisition cost (CAC), sales velocity and revenue per learner.

### Marketing and Demand Generation

- Global Branding: Position CodersTrust as a premier EdTech brand, driving awareness and trust among learners, institutions and partners.
- Performance Marketing: Develop and execute ROI-focused marketing campaigns to generate high-quality leads and convert them effectively.
- Product Positioning: Craft compelling value propositions and messaging that resonate with target audiences across regions.
- Digital Strategy: Leverage digital marketing platforms, data analytics and CRM tools to optimize engagement and conversion rates.

### Customer Success and Retention

- Retention Metrics: Design programs to boost customer lifetime value (CLV) and reduce churn through personalized engagement strategies.
- Success Framework: Build a robust customer success framework that ensures learners achieve their educational and career goals.
- Feedback Utilization: Use data and feedback to improve services, ensuring high satisfaction rates and long-term loyalty.

### Strategic Partnerships and Ecosystem Development

- Corporate Collaborations: Forge partnerships with corporations to offer upskilling and reskilling programs for their workforce.
- Institutional Alliances: Establish alliances with governments, NGOs and educational institutions to drive large-scale adoption of CodersTrust solutions.
- Revenue from Partnerships: Monetize relationships through licensing agreements, co-branded offerings and customized solutions.

### Data-Driven Decision Making

- Revenue Analytics: Implement advanced analytics to track revenue performance, identify trends and inform decision-making.
- Predictive Insights: Leverage predictive models to anticipate market shifts and adapt strategies proactively.
- Continuous Improvement: Establish a culture of experimentation, learning and optimization across all revenue-generating functions.

### Leadership and Organizational Impact

- Team Development: Build, mentor and retain a high-performing team across sales, marketing and customer success functions.
- Cultural Leadership: Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration and accountability aligned with CodersTrust’s mission.
- Cross-Functional Alignment: Work closely with product, operations and finance teams to align on organizational priorities and initiatives.

### Financial Planning and Budget Management

- Budget Ownership: Manage and optimize budgets for marketing, sales and customer success, ensuring cost-effective use of resources.
- Financial Forecasting: Deliver accurate revenue forecasts to support strategic planning and decision-making.
- Profitability Analysis: Identify opportunities to improve profit margins without compromising learner outcomes or organizational values.

### Reporting To

- Chairman
- CEO

### Performance Metrics (KPIs)

- Achievement of quarterly and annual revenue targets.
- Growth in customer acquisition and retention rates.
- Market penetration in new geographies and segments.
- ROI on marketing and sales initiatives.
- Increase in corporate and institutional partnerships.

### Personal Attributes

- Passionate about education and technology.
- Patient and empathetic, with a genuine desire to help others succeed.
- Innovative mindset with a willingness to explore new teaching methods.
- Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.
- Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple mentees.
- Passionate about helping others achieve their career goals. Proactive and resourceful in problem-solving.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Marketing or a related field; MBA or equivalent advanced degree preferred.
- 25+ years of progressive experience in sales, marketing or revenue leadership roles, with 15+ years in the EdTech or technology sector.
- Proven success in scaling revenue streams and achieving growth in global markets.
- Expertise in P&L management, financial modeling and revenue forecasting.
- Strong interpersonal, negotiation and relationship-building skills.
- Data-driven mindset with the ability to translate insights into actionable strategies.
- Must have a clear understanding of basic financial statements including P&L, Cash Flow and Balance Sheet.
- Must have experience of owning and managing P&L and cash flow of any business unit independently.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Experience managing multicultural and cross-functional teams.
- Familiarity with advanced CRM tools, digital marketing platforms and analytics.
- Prior experience in emerging markets or mission-driven organizations.
- Strong entrepreneurial mindset and adaptability to a fast-paced environment.
