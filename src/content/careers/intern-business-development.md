## Your Role and Responsibilities

Your role will be to assist your supervisor in versatile tasks – the successful candidate will have the opportunity to gain valuable experience in various aspects of communication, research, writing, presentation and management. You will play an instrumental role in collaborating with different personnel, projects and departments of the company.

The following list of typical responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

- Conduct research and gather information as needed
- Prepare reports, presentations and other materials
- Collaborate with team members to complete projects and tasks
- Contact external parties, attend meetings and take minutes
- Provide support in writing marketing communications and managing social media
- Assist in developing new offerings from inception to launch, executing various business models
- Assist in developing value proposition, marketing and sales plans and models for new offerings
- Assist in developing PowerPoint presentations, proposals, create workflows, SOPs and guidelines
- Monitor different initiatives of the projects, their progress and completion
- Create and present PowerPoint presentations

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- BBA in Marketing, Management
- Good academic score in O-level and A-level

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Familiarity with customer relationship management software, marketing automation tools and other marketing and sales technologies
