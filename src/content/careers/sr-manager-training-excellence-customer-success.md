## Your Role and Responsibilities

The following list of key responsibility areas (KRAs) and responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

### Competency-Based Course Development and Acquisition Strategy

- Develop and execute a comprehensive CBT&A (Competency-based Training and Assessment), OBE (Outcome-based Education) and Digital Blended Learning strategy aligned with the institute’s global training goals.
- Identify training needs and design programs focusing on developing specific competencies.
- Implement and oversee assessment methodologies to measure the effectiveness of competency-based training programs.
- Identify trends and emerging needs in the training landscape.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to ensure the training strategy is aligned with the evolving needs of the industry.

### Learning Paths and Course Content Development

- Development of comprehensive CBT&A-based training materials, including manuals, guides and multimedia content.
- Collaborate with instructors and content creators to ensure that CBT&A-based training materials are aligned with program objectives.
- Oversee the continuous improvement and evolution of CBT&A-based training curricula and materials based on feedback and evaluation results.
- Lead and Monitor the production team to ensure high-quality content production.

### Instructors and Mentors Development

- Efficiently recruit and manage Instructors and Mentors, including a budget and training facilities, to ensure optimal delivery of Training programs.
- Design and lead Training of Trainers (ToT) programs to equip instructors and team members with the necessary skills and knowledge in delivering effective Competency-Based Training and Assessment.
- Develop comprehensive training materials for ToT programs, emphasizing best practices in CBT&A methodology.
- Provide ongoing support and mentorship to instructors, ensuring continuous improvement in their delivery.
- Monitor the impact of ToT programs on instructor performance and adjust strategies as needed to enhance the overall effectiveness of CBT&A training.

### Training Operations and Outcomes Assurance

- Lead, Maintain and improve training operations for sustained excellence in program delivery, engagement and outcomes.
- Lead and Guide the team to manage the schedules of Instructors and Mentors effectively and optimize resource utilization efficiently.
- Establish and manage a systematic process for collecting feedback from learners on training programs.
- Analyze learner feedback to identify areas of improvement and implement enhancements to training content and delivery.
- Proactively address learner concerns and objections, providing effective solutions and ensuring a positive learning experience.
- Use learner feedback to continuously refine and optimize CBT&A-based training strategies.
- Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure and benchmark training excellence.
- Drive initiatives to continually enhance the quality and relevance of CBT&A-based training programs on traditional and digital platforms.
- Monitor industry trends and advancements to incorporate best practices into CBT&A-based training strategies.

### Customer Experience and Success

- Lead and Facilitate a smooth onboarding process for new clients/learners, ensuring a clear understanding of the organization’s offerings, policies and available resources.
- Lead the team to Collaborate with clients/Learners to identify their specific training needs and customize programs accordingly.
- Lead the team to build and nurture strong relationships with clients/Learners and serving as the main point of contact and addressing any concerns or inquiries promptly.
- Regularly assess the effectiveness of training programs in meeting client/Learners expectations and goals, making adjustments as needed for optimal outcomes.
- Gather feedback from clients/Learners on training experiences, analyze data and provide insights to internal teams for continuous improvement.
- Identify opportunities for upselling additional training programs or services, enhancing the value proposition for clients/Learners.
- Encourage satisfied clients to become advocates, sharing success stories and testimonials that can be used for marketing and promotional activities.
- Address and resolve any issues or conflicts promptly.
- Generate and share regular reports on the impact of training programs, demonstrating the value delivered to clients.
- Develop and implement strategies to enhance client satisfaction, loyalty and long-term partnerships with the training institute.

### Digital Learning Platforms Management

- Lead the digital training platforms to ensure a seamless user-friendly experience.
- Collaborate with IT and design teams to integrate innovative ideas to improve the digital learning environment.
- Closely collaborate with the Instructors and Mentors to incorporate the CBT&A principles while crafting the courses details.
- Development and Acquisition of standard and digital content, including e-learning modules, videos and interactive materials.
- Ensure the global training standards and contents for the digital platform.
- Innovate and implement new ideas that will create the cross sell and up sell for the across all digital platforms.

### Content Acquisition and Platform Onboarding of Academic Institutes, Training Providers and Job Portals

- Identify potential academic institutes, training providers (Digital Learning Platforms) and job portals for collaboration.
- Develop and implement outreach strategies to engage with prospective partners.
- Initiate and lead negotiations with academic institutes, training providers (Digital Learning Platforms) and job portals.
- Collaborate with legal and contractual teams to ensure the development of mutually beneficial agreements.
- Secure necessary approvals and signatures on partnership agreements.
- Design a streamlined onboarding process for new academic institutes and training providers.
- Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration of partner content onto the platform.
- Cultivate strong relationships with key contacts at academic institutes and training providers.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for partners, addressing inquiries and resolving issues promptly.
- Conduct regular check-ins to assess partner satisfaction and identify opportunities for collaboration.
- Stay informed about industry trends and best practices in content acquisition and onboarding.
- Proactively identify opportunities for process improvement and optimization.
- Solicit feedback from partners and internal stakeholders to drive continuous improvement initiatives.

### Corporate Training Solutions

- Understand the training needs and objectives of corporate clients.
- Build strong relationships with key stakeholders within client organizations.
- Conduct needs assessments to identify skill gaps and tailor training solutions accordingly.
- Develop detailed training proposals that outline program objectives, methodologies and deliverables.
- Present proposals to clients, addressing questions and adapting solutions based on client feedback.

### Proposal Development for the Projects

- Thoroughly review and comprehend the RFP documents.
- Conduct a detailed analysis of project specifications, goals and constraints.
- Identify potential ambiguities or gaps in the RFP and seek clarification from relevant stakeholders.
- Craft clear and concise project descriptions, objectives and scope of work.
- Develop a structured and organized RFP document, ensuring all required elements are included.
- Stay updated on relevant regulations and standards impacting RFP development.
- Incorporate necessary disclaimers, terms and conditions to protect the organization’s interests.

### Operational Excellence

- Lead cross-functional teams in identifying and implementing process improvements.
- Develop and maintain key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure operational performance.
- Analyze data to identify trends, root causes of inefficiencies and opportunities for improvement.
- Design and implement lean methodologies and best practices to streamline workflows.
- Facilitate workshops and training sessions to foster a culture of continuous improvement.
- Collaborate with stakeholders at all levels to align improvement efforts with strategic goals.
- Monitor and report on the progress of improvement projects, ensuring deadlines and objectives are met.
- Drive change management initiatives to ensure buy-in and adoption of new processes.
- Conduct regular audits and assessments to identify areas for further optimization.
- Stay informed about industry trends and best practices in operational excellence.
- Ensure all operations are carried on in an appropriate, cost-effective way.
- Improve operational management systems, processes and best practices.
- Purchase materials, plan inventory and oversee warehouse efficiency.
- Help the organization’s processes remain legally compliant.
- Formulate strategic and operational objectives.
- Examine financial data and use them to improve profitability.
- Manage budgets and forecasts.
- Perform quality controls and monitor production KPIs.
- Recruit, train and supervise staff.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Minimum 12+ years experience in the relevant field.
- Bachelor’s degree in Education, Human Resources or a related field.
- Proven experience in designing and implementing CBT&A based training programs.
- Strong analytical skills and a data-driven approach to training optimization.
- Experience with e-learning platforms and digital training solutions.
- Leadership experience with a track record of driving positive training outcomes.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Master’s degree in Education, Human Resources or a related field.
