## Your Role and Responsibilities

We’re looking for a Robotics Mentor with a STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) background who can guide and help students as they explore robotics and related subjects. You will mentor, academically support and guide K-12 kids as they build their skills in robotics engineering and programming principles. This position requires a thorough understanding of robotics principles, programming languages such as C++ and Python, electronic components and related technologies. As a mentor, you must be passionate about science and technology, have strong communication skills and be able to inspire youngsters to pursue STEM careers.

The following list of typical responsibilities you will take on is indicative, not exhaustive. You’ll have a reasonable workload that can assure high-quality results.

- Provide one-on-one or group mentorship to K-12 students, offering guidance, encouragement and support in their STEM studies.
- Demonstrate a strong understanding of science concepts across various STEM disciplines and effectively communicate them to students.
- Assist students in grasping challenging technical concepts, problem-solving techniques and critical thinking skills.
- Collaborate with the Discovery Education and Thimble.io team to develop lesson plans, and educational resources to enhance students’ learning experience.
- Assess students’ strengths and weaknesses and tailor instruction to meet their needs, ensuring a personalized learning experience
- Instruct and train students with hands-on demonstrations of robotic concepts and principles. This may include theoretical explanations and practical demonstrations with assembly and programming languages.
- Provide insight and information about potential STEM career paths, higher education opportunities and the importance of STEM in various industries.
- Inspire and motivate students to explore and pursue STEM fields, promoting curiosity, creativity and a passion for technical and software skills.
- Track and monitor students’ progress, identifying areas for improvement and implementing strategies to help them succeed.
- Collaborate with fellow mentors, educators and parents to create a supportive and enriching learning environment for students.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Robotics, Computer Science, Engineering or a STEM/Science-related field (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Engineering, Mathematics)
- Strong knowledge and understanding of robotics principles, programming language (Python and C++), electronics and mechanical systems
- 2-3 years of experience working with students, particularly children, in an educational institute tutoring or mentoring capacity is necessary.
- Commitment to safety and ethical practices in robotics

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Good understanding and awareness of STEM Education Syllabus
- Knowledge of educational technologies and online learning platforms is an advantage
- Published work would be advantageous
