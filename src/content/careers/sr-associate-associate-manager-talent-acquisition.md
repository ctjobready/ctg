## Your Role and Responsibilities

We are looking for a proactive individual with a strong background in benefits administration, KPI-based project management, organizational development and talent acquisition. As a Sr. Associate/Associate Manager, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the smooth operation of our organization by managing various HR and administrative processes.

### Talent Management

- Participate in the hiring and screening process by posting jobs, reviewing applications, setting up interviews and checking references.
- Organize orientation and onboarding processes for new hires to ensure a smooth transition into the workplace culture.
- Oversee the administration of employee benefit plans, including enrollment, claims handling and answering questions.
- Keep track of personnel records and make sure that corporate policies and applicable laws are followed.
- Encourage the use of performance management procedures, including goal-setting, performance reviews and necessary disciplinary measures.
- Determine targets, goals, KRAs and KPIs to create a system of performance-based evaluation for each employee.
- Help with grievances, employee counseling and dispute resolution in the context of employee relations.
- Need to be competent and effective in headhunting in LinkedIn, FB, etc. for Talent acquisition.

### Administrative Assistance

- Oversee daily administrative tasks, such as maintaining vendor connections, managing office supplies and office expenses.
- Oversee employee lodging and travel arrangements as needed.
- Organize conferences, meetings and events by planning the agenda, handling logistics and scheduling.
- Contribute to the creation and dissemination of internal communications, including announcements, memoranda and newsletters.
- Respond to common questions and offer department-wide administrative assistance to staff members.
- Retain privacy and exercise caution when handling sensitive data.

### Policy Implementation and Compliance

- Keep abreast of pertinent with relevant employment laws and regulations to ensure compliance.
- Contribute to the creation and execution of HR initiatives, policies and practices.
- Participate in HR audits and assist in corrective action plans as needed.

### Payroll and Benefits

- Ensure leave posting and maintaining its record.
- Prepare payroll preparation sheet and guide for necessary checking.
- Prepare worker’s increment and promotion list and keep the approved copy for further record keeping.
- Prepare different payments such as- final settlement, maternity leave benefit bill (prenatal and postnatal), subsistence allowance bill, etc.

## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Human Resource Management, MBA or Post Graduate Diploma (PGD) in Human Resource Management.
- 6/7 years of experience in HR and administrative roles.
- Strong understanding of HR principles, practices and regulations.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to multitask, prioritize and manage time effectively.
- Discretion and ability to handle confidential information appropriately.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Proficiency in HRIS software.
- Knowledge of Bangladesh Labor, Compliance and Industrial Relation Laws.
