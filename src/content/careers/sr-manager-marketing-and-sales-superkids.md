## Your Role and Responsibilities

As the Sr. Manager of Marketing and Sales at SuperKids, you’ll be at the forefront of driving growth and brand excellence. Your strategic vision and leadership will shape our marketing and sales efforts, ensuring alignment with business objectives. Here’s what your role entails:

### Strategic Planning

- Develop and implement comprehensive marketing and sales plans that align with SuperKids’ overall business strategy.
- Use market research to understand customer needs, preferences and buying behavior, translating insights into actionable strategies.

### Budget Management

- Take charge of the marketing budget, ensuring cost-effectiveness while meeting sales targets.
- Allocate resources wisely across various marketing and sales initiatives.

### Campaign Execution

- Lead the execution of marketing campaigns across multiple channels, including digital marketing, social media, email and content marketing.
- Craft compelling messaging that resonates with our audience.

### Sales Strategy

- Collaborate closely with the sales team to develop sales plans and strategies.
- Identify target markets, sales channels and pricing models to drive customer acquisition and retention.

### Market Monitoring

- Stay abreast of market trends, competitor activities and customer feedback.
- Identify growth opportunities and areas for improvement.

### Stakeholder Relationships

- Cultivate and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, including customers, partners, vendors and industry influencers.
- Represent SuperKids with professionalism and integrity.

### Team Leadership

- Mentor and guide the marketing and sales team members.
- Provide training, feedback and support to enhance their performance.

### Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Work closely with cross-functional teams (product development, customer support, finance) to ensure consistent messaging and a seamless customer experience.
