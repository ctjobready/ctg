## Qualifications and Skills

### Mandatory

To qualify to enter the selection process for this job, you must meet the following minimum criteria. Please DO NOT apply if you do not meet these criteria:

- 5+ years of experience in animation, with a strong portfolio showcasing interactive and motion graphics work.
- Bachelor’s degree in Animation, Graphic Design, Multimedia Arts or a related field. Equivalent professional experience will also be considered.
- Proficiency in animation software such as Adobe Animate, Adobe After Effects, Spline or similar tools.
- Experience with 3D animation software like Blender, Maya or Cinema 4D.
- Knowledge of scripting languages such as JavaScript or ActionScript.
- Strong understanding of animation principles and techniques.
- Strong artistic sense, with the ability to create visually appealing animations that align with brand guidelines.
- High attention to detail and a keen eye for design aesthetics.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to articulate animation concepts to non-technical team members.
- Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment, as well as independently.

### Preferred

If you have the following skills/competence, you’ll have a competitive advantage in our selection process compared to others:

- Familiarity with interactive platforms like Unity or Unreal Engine.
- Experience in creating animation prototypes for user testing and feedback.
